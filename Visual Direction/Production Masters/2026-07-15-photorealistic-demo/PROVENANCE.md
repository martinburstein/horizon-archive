Use case: `stylized-concept`
Asset type: premium current-generation first-person game environment plate, 16:9 landscape
Style/medium: maximum-quality cinematic photorealism; feature-film environmental VFX; premium flagship science-fiction game key art; physically based materials and coherent global illumination; one original Builder design language shared across the campaign.
Camera: believable first-person environmental camera, normally a 24–32mm full-frame lens, with clear foreground, interactive middle ground, deep system background, and no visible player.
Builder logic: show inputs, transformation, distribution, return, maintenance, graceful failure, compatible repair, and at least three stewardship eras. Beauty comes from function, scale, time, and collective stewardship.
Continuity palette: mineral ivory, charcoal ceramic, smoky transparent glass, aged bronze/nickel, restrained amber process or geothermal light, cool gray-blue environmental fill, and a natural cinematic grade.
Global constraints: no people, protagonist, hands, body, shadow, reflection, companion, ship, vehicles, human-facing controls, readable text, symbols, UI, watermark, faces, eyes, altars, runes, fantasy crystals, generic neon/cyberpunk, retro pixels, painterly rendering, pseudo-writing, cloned repetition, melted geometry, impossible joints, incoherent reflection, excessive fog, bloom, vignette, or shallow focus used to hide defects.
