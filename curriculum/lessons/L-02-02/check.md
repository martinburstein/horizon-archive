# L-02-02 check and remediation key

The machine-readable key is `answer_key.json`; the two `reference_*_answers.json` files prove the success path.

- `principle`: identify the closest harm, then contrast its nearest neighbor.
- `stakeholder`: name the people experiencing the consequence, not a convenient vendor.
- `mitigation`: require an observable control, test, fallback, appeal, or interface change; a slogan is insufficient.
- `owner`: name a human or organizational role with authority to act; a model, platform, robot, or device cannot be accountable.

A plausible secondary principle is not irrelevant. The assessment asks for the best primary classification for consistent single-best-answer reasoning.
