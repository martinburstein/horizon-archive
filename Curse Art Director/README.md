# Curse Art Director

The Curse Art Director is Horizon Archive's persistent environment-art quality gate. It protects the production richness associated with a late-1990s, hand-authored 640 x 480 adventure while requiring every concrete scene, object, composition, and interaction treatment to remain original to Horizon Archive.
