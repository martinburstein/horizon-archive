# Horizon Archive Art-Direction Charter

The authoritative visual standard is [Photorealistic Visual Direction](../Visual%20Direction/PHOTOREALISTIC_CHARTER.md). Pixel-art requirements were retired on 2026-07-15 and are preserved only in [Pixelated Draft](../Pixelated%20Draft/README.md).

## North star

Horizon Archive should look like a premium contemporary science-fiction production photographed inside real, ancient, functioning alien environments. The images should be convincing enough to invite forensic inspection: materials have mass, weather has depth, glass bends light, heat affects air, repairs reveal time, and immense systems continue beyond the frame.

Classic LucasArts adventures remain a gameplay and staging reference only: memorable locations, readable interaction, compact dialogue, observational humor, and safe experimentation. They are not an art-style target.

## Builder aesthetic doctrine

- Beauty comes from collective purpose, patient maintenance, and accumulated revision—not decoration.
- Show at least three stewardship eras through joints, material aging, reroutes, sealed fabric, repairs, and later adaptations.
- Every strange form must participate in a visible system: input, transformation, distribution, output/return, failure, and maintenance.
- Avoid human ergonomic defaults such as eye-level consoles, chairs, doors, stairs, rails, keyboards, familiar vehicles, and bilateral machine faces.
- Keep at least two plausible functional readings alive. The image may be strange, but it may not be arbitrary.
- Use realistic scale cues from repeated components, fluid/energy paths, atmosphere, erosion, accretion, and maintenance variation—not people or human architecture.

## Glass Garden doctrine

- The ground is absolutely flat to a ruler-straight horizon. Cultivated glass is the only vertical landform.
- Glass crop continues to every edge and toward the horizon. No mountains, ridges, roads, rows, fences, farm buildings, pits, terraces, or raised machinery.
- Fictional silica-rich material rises through flush interlocking growth/cutter systems into tubes, fins, petals, panes, sleeves, guides, and replacement stock.
- Show feed, shaping, maturity, cutting, annealing, routing, rejection, salvage, and repair as parts of one landscape-scale process.
- Render glass photorealistically: variable wall thickness, refraction, caustics, internal reflections, sharp highlights, trapped inclusions, strain, condensation, dust, abrasion, and believable contact shadows.

## Terminal family grammar

- A Terminal is one exposed coupling in a larger distributed Machine; its conduits, buses, fluid paths, or structural continuities leave the frame or enter terrain.
- It must not resemble a human monitor, laptop, kiosk, altar, face, eye, or isolated glowing rock.
- Its artificial nature emerges through precise material relationships, repeated timing, and persistent operation inside geology or infrastructure.
- Diagnostic marks are abstract relationships, not readable glyphs or a translatable alien alphabet.
- For screen-only loops, use one locked master body/camera plate and animate only the defined membrane or emission layer. Lighting changes must be physically consistent and tightly localized.

## Clean-plate rules

- World plates are first-person and contain no protagonist, companion, ship, hands, body, shadow, reflection, portrait, or visible human trace.
- No baked dialogue, cursor, UI, watermark, logo, readable label, arrow, or human-facing instruction.
- Human-readable Python and assessment controls belong to a separate expedition overlay.
- Use a 16:9 composition with clear foreground, middle ground, background, primary interaction silhouette, and route/return readability.
- Preserve functional hierarchy in grayscale and at smaller display sizes; do not depend on color alone.

## Delivery

- Generate and retain the highest-quality source master available.
- Target at least `1920 x 1080` for final 16:9 plates when tooling permits; larger masters are welcome.
- Create responsive display derivatives with high-quality photographic resampling. Never introduce intentional pixelation, nearest-neighbor scaling, palette reduction, or retro dithering.
- Keep world art, UI, and animation as separable layers where interaction or reduced-motion behavior requires it.
- Record prompt, model/tool, source references, date, edits, crop, and derivative history in the provenance log.
- A plate passes only when its materials, lighting, scale, function, first-person framing, interaction hierarchy, originality, and artifact-free finish survive live review.
