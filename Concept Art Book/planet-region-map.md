## Traversal map for the playable prologue

`GM-00 Landing Shelf → GM-01 Glass Meadow → AB-01 Drowned Archive → WC-01 Witness Corridor → SC-01 City Overlook`

This line records player-facing progression only. It does not assert that the locations are physically adjacent or define the travel mechanism between worlds.

## Expansion rule

Add a world only when it has a distinct learning purpose, traversal identity, and visual climate. Do not create planets merely to increase the count.
