# Builder Machine Signal-Coupler Terminal Prompt

This is the current durable art-direction authority for the derelict Terminal sprite. It deliberately moves two conceptual steps beyond the original signal-mineral study: the contact is now one exposed diagnostic organ of a much larger buried Machine, its membrane uses cold scientific topology rather than warning-red pseudo-writing, and its silhouette is precision-grown infrastructure rather than a rock with a screen.

```text
Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: Horizon Archive six-frame production-pixel sprite exploration sheet for a Builder Machine diagnostic coupler; intended final reduction is six consistent 64 x 64 square-logical-pixel frames
Primary request: Design an original, low asymmetric signal-coupler head that is visibly continuous with a vast scientific Machine buried below and extending beyond the crop. It is not a standalone Terminal, shrine, mineral prop, or human console. Interlock purpose-grown glass ceramic, dark indigo technical substrate, precision repair bands, and one clipped nonhuman instrument membrane.
Subject: one half-buried coupling organ with a broad low body, offset glass-ceramic vanes, sectional maintenance joints, and a small off-center horizontal clipped-polygon membrane integrated into the collar. Preserve the broad laminated front tongue as a multi-lane data bus that continues through the bottom crop. Extend paired sleeved phase channels through both side crops. Route secondary conduits through sealed collars and visibly beneath the ground. Every connection must look load-bearing and functional, never decorative or loose-ended.
Animation states, reading left-to-right then top-to-bottom: 1) nearly dark cold field with one faint cyan seed node; 2) a thin blue-white spectral scan crossing an empty field; 3) an abstract nested node topology made from offset dots and broken orthogonal traces; 4) two scientific phase contours displaced out of register; 5) dense cyan/blue-white interference and sparse cold static; 6) topology collapsing to a dim field with one tiny amber fault tick. Style/medium: hand-authored-looking late-1990s point-and-click adventure pixel art at native low logical resolution; square logical pixels, crisp stepped diagonals, deliberate chunky clusters, one-pixel accents, restrained dithering; scientifically designed but ancient, repaired, and nonhuman. Futurism comes from precision, layered materials, phase routing, distributed-system evidence, and graceful failure rather than glossy cyberpunk styling.
```

## Interconnection acceptance gate

At thumbnail size, the object must read as a cropped portion of an unseen network. The front tongue continues through the bottom boundary as a laminated data bus; sleeved channels continue through both side boundaries; at least one secondary conduit passes through a sealed collar and beneath the substrate. No rail, wire, route, or tongue may terminate decoratively inside the sprite.

## Implementation timing

Recommended loop durations are `620, 170, 260, 240, 150, 500 ms`. Runtime may add an irregular `800-1300 ms` dark hold. No body plane, glass vane, bus, cable sleeve, docking seam, contact shadow, or debris may pulse.

## Production reduction gate

Generated work remains exploration until deliberately reduced to `64 x 64`, given hard alpha edges, checked at native size and exact nearest-neighbor magnification, and validated for byte-stable body and connections outside the membrane. Reject any cell that changes silhouette, camera, root placement, or machine continuity.
