# Builder Machine Signal-Coupler — Photorealistic Prompt V3

Status: **ACTIVE**

## Image-generation prompt

```text
Use case: cinematic photorealistic science-fiction environment asset
Asset type: one high-resolution registered master plate for a derelict Builder Machine diagnostic coupling embedded in a larger landscape system, 16:9 composition, no UI

Primary request: Create an original, physically convincing alien signal-coupler that is visibly one exposed organ of a vast scientific Machine buried below the terrain and continuing far beyond the crop. It is not a standalone Terminal, shrine, mineral prop, or human console. The object has a broad low asymmetric body formed from laminated dark indigo technical ceramic, weathered translucent glass-ceramic vanes, precise repair bands from multiple stewardship eras, sealed fluid/phase collars, and one small off-center clipped-polygon diagnostic membrane. A broad clean laminated tongue extends from the front as a flush multi-lane structural/data bus with no tube on top. Major conduits leave both side crops and continue underground or far off-screen; every connection is load-bearing and functional.

Environment and age: half-buried in an ancient functioning alien industrial ecology, with mineral accretion, dust, micro-abrasion, condensation, embedded residue, settled debris, and later repairs physically integrated into the body. The object remains alive and quietly performing one persistent task after its civilization vanished. It is melancholy through continued function, not through horror or supernatural cues.

Photorealism: premium contemporary cinematic VFX realism; physically based materials; ray-traced-quality global illumination; realistic glass thickness, refraction, internal reflection, caustics, edge wear, inclusions, and dust; dark technical ceramic with micro-scratches and thermal staining; believable contact shadows and ground occlusion; restrained volumetric atmosphere; sharp high-resolution surface detail with a physical cause. No illustration, painting, pixel-art texture, low-poly faceting, retro dithering, CRT effect, neon cyberpunk gloss, or generic fantasy crystal styling.

Camera and composition: first-person three-quarter environmental view, low object occupying the central/lower field, landscape context visible, clean tongue continuing through the lower crop, side/underground connections clearly legible, no tactical isometric angle. Preserve useful negative space for separate game UI. The diagnostic membrane is discoverable but subordinate to the whole coupled system.

Membrane: one dark clipped-polygon scientific surface with faint cold cyan/blue-white topology. Marks express node spacing, discontinuous routes, phase contours, scan density, and incomplete lattices only. No letters, numerals, words, runes, repeated glyph blocks, warning triangles, familiar icons, face, eye, or translatable language. One tiny amber fault accent is allowed; no red, crimson, magenta, or green emission.

Constraints: absolutely no protagonist, people, humanoids, hands, ship, character, portrait, reflection of a person, HUD, caption, label, logo, watermark, human controls, buttons, keyboard, desk, chair, kiosk, circular display, face-like symmetry, mushroom, magic altar, floating hologram, loose familiar cable, readable text, or conventional monitor. Original design only; do not reproduce the No Man's Sky reference object's oval silhouette, screen shape, marks, palette, environment, or composition.

Quality rejection: no melted seams, duplicated conduits, impossible tangencies, floating fragments, inconsistent shadows, incoherent reflections, pseudo-writing, warped repeated geometry, overbloom, excessive depth of field, or meaningless greeble.
```

## Animation process

1. Generate and approve one high-resolution master body/environment plate.
2. Define a fixed membrane mask and, if needed, a second tightly bounded local-light-spill mask.
3. Produce six states from that same master: dark hold, seed node, spectral scan, incomplete topology, displaced phase contours, interference/fault tick.
4. Composite states without changing any pixel outside the approved masks.
5. Verify body/camera/environment identity by difference image and hash outside the masks.
6. Provide a reduced-motion still using the quiet seed-node state.

The six frames are not separately regenerated scenes. Exact registration is a production requirement independent of rendering style.
