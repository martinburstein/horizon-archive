## Builder garden/masterwork doctrine

Glass Meadow is not a naturally enchanted crystal garden. It is a civic-scale fictional Builder material farm whose glass grows upward from flush prepared ground cells like inverted icicles across a perfectly level field. Generations cultivate purpose-shaped glassware for the Python-running Terminals. This is project canon, not a claim about real-world glass science. The wider mystery remains unresolved, but feedstock, water, cultivation, component use, access, and maintenance must visibly depend on one another.

### Selected material and process synthesis

- Preserve the selected perfectly flat infinite horizon and semi-plausible floor: a continuous matte mineral substrate with fine pores, hairline fissures, embedded process seams, and restrained thermal/moisture variation, never relief.
- Fictional silica magma escapes through those floor apertures into flush, interlocking alien cutter/growth mats and rises as purpose-grown tubes. Show at least one readable feed-to-root-to-tube relationship.
- Mats preserve new growth, regulate heat and stress, train geometry, mark maturity, and cut or release harvests. They stay flush, have no human-facing front or controls, and create orderly alien phase/branching relationships rather than crop rows or grids.
- Glass reads as realistic glass through variable wall thickness, transparent and translucent passages, refraction, internal reflection, sharp edge highlights, subtle bubbles/inclusions or strain, believable contact shadows, and selective surface wear. Do not render generic faceted crystals, opaque plastic, neon plumbing, or magical glow.
- These visual material cues may be plausible; the silica-magma growth and harvest system remains fictional Builder technology.

- **Collective work:** the feedstock system, growth field, Petal, dormant state band, subsurface water control, stress/temperature system, embedded annealing/transport, and repair stock form one maintained public work rather than unrelated scenic props.
- **Longitudinal phases:** show older flush collar geometries and subsurface distribution signatures; a later Petal-era process graft; and newer repair glass, altered training relationships, and reactivated capillary states—all without elevation.
- **Functional sublime:** the Petal array, flush collar states, capillary moisture signatures, conformal guides, and crop-state waves visibly exchange light, water, support, or signal. Its scale comes from coordinating the flat field, not standing above it as a monument.
- **Civic/ritual use:** express collective stewardship through immense synchronized state waves, repeated care intervals, and compatible changes across component families. Do not add gathering platforms, processional paths, or skyline structures.
- **Maintenance ornament:** use graft collars, inspection seams, drainage combs, replacement plates, alignment notches, scaffold sockets, and hand-varied pruning frames as the scene's decorative rhythm.
- **Accumulated revisions:** retain dormant subsurface branches, mismatched flush collars, low repair stock, altered field relationships, and negotiated joins between eras without berms, trenches, terraces, or visible roads.
- **Engineered agriculture:** every glass growth rises from a prepared collar or seed form and follows slurry/water, temperature/stress, shape-training, maturity, harvest, or Terminal-component infrastructure. Organic life remains distinct. No random crystal scatter.
- **Visual school:** classify the meadow as the Horizon Field School—absolute planar continuity, crop-borne light management, non-Cartesian process topology, vast state waves, and proudly visible repair differences. This is a production label, not hidden lore or player-facing exposition.

### Absolute-flatness composition lock

- Terrain is one perfectly level plane with a ruler-straight horizon from edge to edge.
- Glass crop continues to every lateral edge and into atmospheric compression at the horizon. Cultivated glass is the only vertical landform.
- No hill, mountain, terrace, ridge, shelf, raised monument, skyline architecture, valley, bowl, basin, overlook, cliff, island, berm, ramp, or platform.
- Depth comes only from atmospheric compression, occlusion, maturity/component-shape variation, diminishing cluster scale/density, and broad waves of process-state.
- Field organization is non-Cartesian and nonhuman: no crop rows, furrows, roads, paths, lanes, fences, hedges, ditches, farm buildings, or familiar field parcels.
- Petal and Route contacts remain crop-continuous; neither stands on a pedestal or breaks the horizon as architecture.

### Alien functionality and first-contact brief

- **Landscape system:** feedstock and slurry enter the beds; collars and training fields transform material; mature components leave through annealing/transport; reject glass and recovered heat/material re-enter earlier stages; bed activity visibly modifies local water, temperature, ground chemistry, and organic cover.
- **Nested scales:** collar/component growth; field cultivation and quality routing; plain-scale resource and microclimate transformation.
- **Nonhuman affordances:** growth controls are spatially distributed, multi-height, variably oriented, and coupled across beds. Petal and Route Marker have no front, screen, seat, hand control, or human-height operating position.
- **Discoverable clues:** repeat a collar/conformal-guide grammar across component families; contrast growing/mature/rejected geometry; show one broad state wave propagating through moisture, embedded thermal control, crop maturity, and downstream sorting without rows or routes.
- **Plausible first readings:** material cultivation, terrain stabilization, atmospheric/light regulation, and distributed computation remain compatible with the visible evidence. “Glass farmer” is not a confirmed Builder occupation title.
- **First contact:** the Python workspace is an expedition translation layer reached through the local node, not a human-facing interface built into the artifact. No prior human modification or presence appears.
