# SC-13 — Measured Horizon

## Scene purpose

Final expedition-owned review at the unchanged RP-011 outer margin. The world remains the accepted SC-12 panorama; no destination, chamber, tribunal, monument, Builder interface, or response appears. A separate suit overlay organizes complete evidence and fresh transfer while preserving a quiet, indifferent first-person horizon.

## Compact boards

1. `MH-00 ASSEMBLE` — unchanged panorama; the suit identifies five separate record scopes, objective version, review action, and separate return.
2. `MH-10 FRESH TRANSFER` — world plate and clocks remain stable; a non-baked expedition work surface replaces only the interface region. Landscape, color, and motion supply no answer.
3. `MH-20 DECISION` — one neutral layout supports ready and not-yet-ready with equal scale, typography, dignity, contrast, and navigation. Remediation exposes routes without answers; ready exposes the no-exam guarantee without spectacle.
4. `MH-30 CLOSE + REVIEW` — the local decision and audit remain reopenable; evidence scopes and remediation are individually navigable; credits follow only through a separate action. The horizon never opens, brightens, moves, or acknowledges completion.

## Production plan

- Reuse the eventual accepted lossless SC-12 panorama as the sole world master; do not generate a near-duplicate ending plate.
- Produce one independent transparent expedition-interface master at maximum practical resolution with desktop, narrow, text-zoom, forced-color, and reduced-motion variants. Never bake it into the world.
- First-person framing excludes protagonist, ship, hands, body, shadow, reflection, human trace, readable Builder text, and in-world human UI.
- World/interface separation is explicit: the landscape owns no verdict; the suit owns labels, evidence, remediation, save, and outcome.
- The optional CRT-inspired shell cannot impose a fixed legacy resolution.

## Composition and accessibility

- The uninterrupted horizon remains dominant behind a centered readable review column. Avoid seals, altars, gates, trophies, red/green binaries, fireworks, sirens, and shame treatment.
- Ready and not-yet-ready share geometry; explicit text and state labels carry meaning.
- Desktop contains the complete world/status/current action in one `100dvh` shell. Narrow and 200% text use vertical reflow, no horizontal escape, complete scope headings, logical heading-first focus, and `>=44 x 44 CSS px` targets.
- Complete text equivalents identify the unchanged world, review owner, objective version, evidence count, gate, local outcome, no-exam guarantee, and remediation/return actions.
- Screen reader, speech, switch, pointer, touch, Enter, and Space preserve meaning. No hidden-scope memory is required.

## Rejection and production gate

- Reject any outcome-dependent horizon, light, weather, clock, or Builder-work change.
- Reject Builder/Machine judgment, portal/open-door imagery, certification/exam branding, readable alien verdicts, school/lab/trial metaphors, or successor vistas.
- Reject baked UI, low-resolution overlays, pixelation, destructive crops, stretch, or artificial blur.
- Final production requires provenance, crop and alpha-edge inspection, text-safe zones, original-size review, and cleanup.
