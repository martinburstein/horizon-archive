The manifest records `71.26%` minimum linear detail retention, one body hash, six distinct membrane hashes, and no animated pixel outside the membrane.

The membrane remains subordinate to the body and does not become a modern monitor.

## Failed gates

### Alien functionality and larger-Machine interconnection

The side extensions technically reach both crop edges, but continuity alone is not enough. Four parallel cyan runs inside black rectangular rails, closed at regular intervals by identical upright clamps, reproduce the visual grammar of human cable tray, busbar, fence, or data trunking. The extensions remain continuously exposed at one height, cross in front of the glass crop, and show no field handoff, branch, buried interval, process-state consequence, or destination relationship. The single front-right ground conduit helps, but reads as an attached hose or probe rather than one scale of a distributed subsurface system.

The result supports only `large object with long cables`, not three nested operating scales. It does not yet show how local membrane activity relates to a site process or the landscape cultivation system.

### Material integration and square-pixel craft

The authored central body has layered indigo planes, seams, selective highlights, glass refraction, and irregular repair evidence. The edge extensions collapse to flat black bands, uniform cyan stripes, repeated rectangular sleeves, and long CAD-straight runs. At `320 x 180`, those runs become the strongest horizontal graphic in the frame and read as a pasted overlay rather than matter passing through the same Meadow substrate, glass crop, atmosphere, and maintenance history.

This is not a resolution failure: the native object retains fine detail. It is a craft and hierarchy mismatch between a hand-shaped central body, a richly painted field, and mechanically repeated edge geometry.

### Theatrical composition and scene dominance

The coupler should be the opening interaction anchor, but the current exposed rails divide the plate into upper and lower halves from edge to edge. Their uninterrupted contrast competes with the ruler-straight horizon and suppresses depth through the crop. The central body may remain large; the side continuities need occlusion, depth change, and substrate entry so the Glass Meadow remains an immense working landscape rather than a background behind one foreground machine.

### Originality

The core body is original, but the added interconnection shorthand is too familiar to satisfy the Builder doctrine. Regular clamps plus parallel glowing lines are a generic human/sci-fi solution that could be removed from Horizon Archive without losing any project-specific meaning. The Machine should communicate interdependence through cultivated material, non-Cartesian routing, repairs, field contacts, and partial burial rather than familiar cable hardware.

## Precise next action

Keep the central high-resolution body, glass fins, clean front laminate, fixed membrane polygon, six membrane states, body hashes, and current first-person placement. Re-author only the conduit continuities and their immediate ground contacts:

1. Preserve each existing body-side collar, then break the four-line parallel bundle within roughly `40–80` native pixels of the housing.
2. Route one side continuity beneath the substrate through two visible flush entry collars; let only short offset glass-ceramic trace windows reappear farther out.
3. Route the opposite continuity behind and between cultivated glass with at least two crop occlusions, a shallow depth change, and one asymmetric branch into a flush growth-mat contact before the remaining line exits the frame.
4. Replace evenly spaced upright cable clamps with three visibly different sealed repair joints from separate stewardship periods. No fence posts, ladder rails, zip ties, loose wire ends, or repeated Cartesian spacing.
5. Reuse the coupler's indigo substrate, pearl glass, cyan diagnostic accent, and Meadow floor pickup so every exposed segment shares the central body's material hierarchy. Keep cyan below the brightness and edge density of the membrane.
6. Keep all conduit pixels frozen across the six frames. Only the existing membrane may animate.

Rebuild both composites and re-submit them at native size. The acceptance check is visual, not merely alpha continuity: at thumbnail scale the viewer should infer `one exposed contact in a field-scale buried system`, while the immense cultivated Meadow still reads beyond it.
