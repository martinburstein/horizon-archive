# CRT-centered Glass Meadow presentation review — 2026-07-13

**Verdict: PASS**

## Outcome

The final `1280 × 720` presentation satisfies the user's priorities. The realistic Glass Meadow remains the dominant image, with its ruler-straight horizon, dense transparent crop, warm floor reflections, and alien growth-mat network still legible. The small centered CRT is a cute, restrained period frame rather than a competing focal object.

Evidence: [final 1280 × 720 presentation](../../horizon-archive-game/qa/glass-meadow-crt-final-1280x720.png) and [source/presentation comparison](../../horizon-archive-game/qa/glass-meadow-crt-comparison.png).

## Gate results

- **Scene richness — pass:** the presentation preserves realistic glass thickness, reflections, translucency, crop density, and immense flat-field depth. Minor display softness does not erase the approved material treatment or focal hierarchy.
- **Composition — pass:** the Meadow occupies the CRT screen decisively; the bezel and surrounding darkness focus rather than crowd it. The objective banner stays clear of the primary foreground silhouettes.
- **Period staging — pass:** the generic dark monitor housing, amber indicators, and physical controls suggest a late-1990s computer without reproducing a protected adventure-game interface or recognizable commercial monitor design.
- **Viewpoint and originality — pass:** the view remains first-person with no protagonist, companion, or ship. No protected characters, scenery, props, icons, or copied composition appear.
- **Interface hierarchy — pass:** location/status, objective, landscape, verbs, dialogue, and inventory remain ordered and separable at the approved almost-full-screen size.
- **Responsive treatment — pass:** browser-native layout zoom fills the display without the former compositor transform; the Meadow retains `image-rendering: auto`. Compact displays switch to the larger narrow layout without a miniature bezel instead of shrinking canonical content inside the shell.

## Accepted guardrails

- Keep the current no-filter treatment: no added scanlines, curvature, glow, chromatic aberration, or blur over the Meadow or interface.
- Preserve the centered restrained bezel, almost-full-screen sizing, first-person/no-ship scene, and smooth realistic glass rendering.
- Re-review only if the bezel grows substantially, a CRT effect obscures text/material detail, hotspot alignment changes, or the selected Meadow raster is replaced.

No art-direction correction is required for the current CRT presentation.
