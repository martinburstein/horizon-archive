1. AI-901 objectives are the master learning goals. AI-900 material may support overlapping fundamentals but never overrides the current AI-901 study guide.
2. Every required Python skill receives one justified primary teaching home before later retrieval or transfer. A new packet may not duplicate a primary home merely to fill a reserved row.
3. Every current official AI-901 objective must have traceable primary, retrieval, remediation, and genuinely fresh-transfer evidence before `RP-012` can pass.
4. A `SOLIDIFIED` mapping reopens only when official objectives materially change, a validator or playtest proves mismatch or bypass, or Martin changes the learning goal.
5. Presentation, viewport fit, world display, lore observation, navigation, acknowledgement, focus, confidence, timing, save, restore, retry, and Tour traversal never substitute for scored mastery evidence.
6. Observation evidence, Python evidence, AI-901 evidence, review eligibility, and save eligibility remain separate unless an approved machine-readable contract explicitly requires their conjunction. Conjunction never permits cross-credit.
7. Remediation must address the missed skill, case, or dimension without revealing transfer answers; the subsequent transfer form must be genuinely blank and independently scored.
8. Store only allowlisted finalized evidence. Clear private and transient working content at the defined boundaries; never store credentials, endpoints, payloads, responses, private notes, identity content, source content, or exam-item text.
9. All learner-facing packages remain offline-first and no-authority: no live Azure or Foundry call, resource access, credential use, external action, access grant, city response, or exam guarantee.
10. Accessibility and recovery are required delivery contracts but remain zero mastery evidence. Required controls, labels, field associations, errors, focus, non-color meaning, reduced motion, and reflow must survive the active presentation.
11. Reserved rows authorize no objective assignment, Python skill, case, answer, threshold, source claim, lore, location, art, route, code, or successor. They reserve order and coverage responsibility only.
12. Volatile official facts—objectives, weights, UI labels, SDK versions, endpoints, authentication, product availability, and scoring policy—must follow the official source register and its re-verification rules at the point of use.
