## Canon decision

The forty lesson clusters form one continuous walking expedition. The Pilot
begins already on foot in the Glass Garden Margin, follows physically open
material continuities through Glass Meadow and the Drowned Archive, enters the
Witness Complex, descends into the City Beneath, and thereafter keeps walking
through the twelve released city locations. No inter-location flight,
teleportation, Machine transport, newly opened door, or post-ending journey is
part of this canon.

Legacy scene-sheet labels `World A`, `World B`, `World C`, and `World D` remain
internal production-region buckets; they are not player-facing evidence of four
planets and do not authorize an off-screen flight. For TE-001 continuity, Glass
Meadow is a cultivated outer civic work, Drowned Archive is its adjoining
flooded process basin, Witness Corridor is the transition complex, and the City
Beneath is the subsurface civic depth of the same connected expedition. “The
city” therefore includes its engineered margins and landscape-scale works, not
only architecture that looks urban to a human.

The word **Terminal** remains expedition shorthand for two separate things:

1. a location-specific physical coupling through which the suit can sample a
   bounded, sanitized local work image; and
2. the stable human-readable interface that the suit renders after connection.

The physical host may be a collar, cavity, service laminate, pressure chamber,
projection relationship, relay fragment, or replica cradle. It never contains
Python, a human screen, readable Builder writing, or an answer. The identical
interface is the suit's mediation, not a repeated Builder monitor design.

## Campaign-wide narrative spine

The route moves from **recognition** to **comparison** to **epistemic restraint**.
In the meadow, the Pilot learns that radically different objects can expose the
same compatible local signal. The Drowned Archive expands that insight across
one immense working basin. Witness Corridor makes the error of resemblance
explicit: the striking fallen assembly is not automatically the coupling. The
city then presents increasingly difficult relationships among visible traces,
missing evidence, different scales, ambiguous correspondences, unfamiliar
materials, and bounded readiness. What changes is the Pilot's method and the
suit's organized evidence, never the silent world.

The walk is motivated by ordinary expedition reasoning:

- a visible process continuation gives the Pilot a direction;
- the suit can compare a newly sampled local signature with nearby emissions;
- existing maintenance, flow, heat, deposition, and material relationships
  make one next inspection more informative than another; and
- the Pilot deliberately chooses to continue.

None of those facts means the Machine arranged a lesson trail, recognized the
Pilot, or issued an invitation.

## Discovery language

| Classification | Canon meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Pilot-first** | The physical anomaly is visually conspicuous before the suit identifies compatibility. |
| **Suit-first** | The host blends into local work; the suit detects a bounded compatible signature before the Pilot would recognize it as usable. |
| **Convergent** | The Pilot notices something strange while the suit independently confirms a compatible local coupling. |

After a cluster, the suit may say that another **compatible local signature** is
within sensor range. It may not say that the Machine wants, expects, permits, or
directs the Pilot to continue. Route copy should use `bearing`, `candidate`,
`correspondence`, `local signal`, or `inspection`, never `summons`, `invitation`,
`quest`, `authorization`, or `unlock`.

## Walking rules

- Every physical host below is reachable before its exercise is completed.
- Traversal uses dry process boundaries, incidental low-growth bands, stable
  foundation exposures, broad inclines, load-bearing margins, service detours,
  and open mineral substrate. These are not human roads or Builder corridors
  designed for a human body.
- No required move includes swimming, jumping a gap, climbing loose rubble,
  touching a live current, crossing an unsafe membrane, or operating machinery.
- A host may be dormant or difficult to recognize, but it is never physically
  sealed behind the lesson that precedes it.
- Completion changes only expedition evidence and interface availability. The
  sole inherited exception is the already-released Glass Meadow Route Marker,
  whose awake/crowned state remains as built. No later host copies that world-
  side progression behavior.
- “Forward” always means a pre-existing, reversible physical continuation plus
  an explicit Pilot decision. Every city location also retains its accepted
  backward route.
