## Player-facing purpose and emotional promise

The document must let a reader experience Horizon Archive as one continuous
first-person pilgrimage: a mortal human learns to use real technical methods
inside an abandoned city whose beauty, care, and silence become progressively
harder to interpret. The reader should understand why every step follows the
last, feel the learner's struggle without seeing an answer key, and reach an
ending whose restraint is more affecting than a spectacle would be.

The intended chill comes from a final inversion: after spending the whole game
trying to understand the Builders and the Machine, the only complete measure
available is of the Pilot's own readiness. The city never gives a verdict.

## Exact entry and permitted exits

### Entry

- The reader begins on foot in the Glass Garden Margin.
- The Pilot has mortal longing, incomplete surface knowledge, and no confirmed
  category for the first coupling.
- There is no visible ship, body, companion, prior human trace, Machine greeting,
  decoded Builder language, or hidden-lore answer.

### Internal exits

- A pass finalizes approved local evidence and permits the same-rail forward
  walk when all independent local gates are satisfied.
- A miss enters answer-free remediation and a genuinely fresh retry in the same
  cluster.
- Close/return restores the same host and exact predecessor continuity.
- Location completion permits reversible pedestrian return without altering
  accepted evidence or world state.

### Final exit

The only final state is the unchanged Measured Horizon after READY or NOT YET
READY, with equal dignity, exact review/remediation access, a no-exam guarantee,
no world response, and `successor=null`.

## Chapter content contract

- opening image and sensory identity;
- the Pilot's current emotional assumption;
- physical walking line through the location;
- why each host belongs in that material system;
- who notices it first and how the suit confirms compatibility;
- the physical connection beat and stable suit-interface transition;
- human-readable learning pressure with no solution or answer cue;
- how a miss feels and recovers without punishment or story loss;
- what a pass records without Machine/world response;

- an exit image whose physical continuity exactly matches the next arrival.

Chapters may condense repeated mechanical language after the rail contract is
established, but every Terminal must retain distinct physical, learning, and
dramatic purpose.
