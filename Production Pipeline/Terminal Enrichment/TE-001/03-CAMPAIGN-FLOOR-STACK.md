## Selected slice and purpose

Create one root-level Markdown artifact, `HORIZON_ARCHIVE_GAME_ON_PAPER.md`,
that can be read straight through like a cinematic novella or shooting script.
It must let a director, writer, designer, or player understand the complete
game without opening production packets.

The artifact must answer four questions at every location:

1. What does the Pilot see and feel on arrival?
2. Why does the Pilot walk to each Terminal in this order?
3. How do practice, failure, remediation, and success affect pacing without
   branching the story or changing the world?
4. What new understanding follows the Pilot into the next location?

## Rail model

Horizon Archive has one canonical physical and story path. It has no alternate
location order, morality branch, fail ending, success ending variant, dialogue
tree, or optional route that changes canon.

The learner state supplies the only meaningful branch:

```text
ARRIVE -> OBSERVE -> CONNECT -> PRACTICE
                                | PASS -> RECORD -> WALK FORWARD
                                | MISS -> BOUNDED FEEDBACK -> REMEDIATE
                                                     -> FRESH RETRY --^
```

- A miss never removes story access already earned, changes city state, or
  creates shame, punishment, death, rejection, or a bad ending.
- A pass never grants Machine favor, opens a physical gate, proves an
  interpretation, or makes scenery celebrate.
- The practical difference is time and attention: a learner who misses stays
  with the idea longer; a learner who passes proceeds.
- Story observations, Python evidence, AI-901 evidence, and save eligibility
  remain independent and never cross-credit.

## Exact entry state

- First-person view already on foot inside GM-00's low-growth state band.
- No visible protagonist body, ship, companion, prior human trace, mission
  giver, Machine greeting, or decoded Builder language.
- The Pilot knows only that the Builders appear to have possessed time,
  continuity, beauty, and care beyond ordinary human reach—and vanished.
- The Pilot's emotional premise is mortal longing: *They had what we ache for.
  Why was it not enough?*
- The suit can detect environmental conditions but has not yet learned the
  local compatible coupling signature.

## Ordered milestones

1. Recognize the first compatible contact without knowing its category.
2. Learn that physical hosts vary while the suit-rendered interface remains
   stable.
3. Complete the Glass/Drowned/Witness sixteen-cluster foundation pilgrimage.
4. Descend into an operating city that does not react to arrival.
5. Build an expedition method across exposed, different, recurring, ordered,
   occluded, ambiguous, and unavailable evidence.
6. Complete every Python and AI-901 cluster through independent local evidence,
   including any required remediation and fresh transfer.
7. Finalize fresh Unborrowed Reach evidence before reopening prior scopes.
8. Stop at the unchanged outer margin and complete the cumulative fresh
   transfer plus all-current-objective readiness review.
9. Close on READY or NOT YET READY with equal dignity, exact remediation access,
   no exam guarantee, no world response, and no successor.

## Checkpoint and return rhythm

- Working content remains transient inside the current Terminal.
- Finalized private-free evidence may save only at its approved boundary.
- Escape/close returns to the same physical host with focus restored; it does
  not abandon the campaign rail.
- Failure returns to answer-free remediation and a genuinely fresh retry inside
  the same lesson cluster.
- Location completion permits reversible pedestrian return along the exact
  predecessor chain. Return never erases accepted evidence or changes canon.
- Final readiness remediation may reopen the exact deficient evidence scope.
  It does not teleport the Pilot in fiction; record review is suit-owned, and a
  chosen physical revisit uses the same walking route.

## Dramatic pacing requirements

The existing rail is logically coherent but risks emotional flatness if every
location is presented as another neutral evidence set. The script must supply
escalation without inventing world responses:

1. **Awe:** the first host is physically strange but usable.
2. **Abundance:** the basin makes compatible contacts seem almost everywhere.
3. **Misrecognition:** Witness proves the human-looking object is not privileged.
4. **Indifference:** the city is alive with maintenance but not with welcome.
5. **Seduction of pattern:** recurrence and convergence tempt interpretation.
6. **Fracture of certainty:** divergence, sealed cases, and missing intervals
   make the easy story fail.
7. **Discipline:** edge evidence and counterexamples teach the Pilot to speak
   more carefully.
8. **Inversion:** the final instrument measures the Pilot's evidence, not the
   Builders.
9. **Quiet ending:** the world withholds spectacle. The emotional release comes
   from earned self-knowledge and the unanswered horizon.

## Required versus optional scope

### Required

- Exact route, cluster ownership, objective order, ending, and no-authority
  boundaries.
- Enough prose to read as one coherent dramatic experience rather than a design
  index.
- Visible failure loops that affect pace and internal effort only.
- Strong inter-chapter transition sentences so the rail feels inevitable but
  never Machine-authored.
- Distinct emotional function for all fifteen locations.

### Optional

- Short director's notes on sound, silence, cut rhythm, or camera emphasis.
- Suggested Pilot recorder lines where surface authority allows.
- Section epigraphs written as original Pilot reflections.

### Forbidden

- New lore answers, a speaking Machine, Builder dialogue, decoded native text,
  humanoid Builder confirmation, world response, physical reward, branching
  canon, alternate ending, failure ending, RP-013, successor, post-ending scene,
  live service call, exam prediction, image work, or media change.
