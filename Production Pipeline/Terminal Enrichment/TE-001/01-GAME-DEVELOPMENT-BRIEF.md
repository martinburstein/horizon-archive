## Audience, promise, and purpose

Horizon Archive remains for an adult learner seeking real zero-to-Python and
AI-901 readiness through serious, accessible first-person science-fiction
play. The player promise is now made more legible, not changed: the Pilot
traverses the already-approved route on foot, recognizes or is alerted to
physically distinct local couplings, connects through one stable suit-rendered
interface, and becomes more capable while the abandoned world remains strange,
silent, and unresponsive.

The purpose of TE-001 is to turn the approved forty-cluster learning map into
one coherent canon walkthrough. It must explain why every host belongs where it
is, how it is safely reachable on foot, who first notices it, why the Pilot
continues to the next host, and how each location rejoins the existing route.
It changes no learning objective, readiness threshold, story packet, or ending.

## Experience pillars

1. **A continuous expedition.** Every move is an intelligible first-person
   walk through already-approved adjacent locations; no spaceship, fast travel,
   teleport, or newly invented route is required.
2. **Strange hosts, stable tool.** No two physical coupling hosts need look the
   same. Their common interface exists only as the suit's readable expedition
   overlay and therefore remains visually and functionally consistent.
3. **Discovery, not invitation.** The Pilot may notice an anomaly, the suit may
   detect the bounded compatible signal, or both may converge. Completion may
   improve the suit's local diagnosis of a nearby coupling, but the Machine
   never summons, rewards, unlocks, or guides the Pilot.
4. **Learning without counterfeit authority.** Real Python and AI-901 practice
   remains separate from scenery, route progress, world state, Microsoft or
   Azure access, and exam authority.
5. **Mystery through material specificity.** Each host must arise from the
   location's approved material systems and functional landscape without
   proving Builder identity, purpose, language, or hidden lore.

## Global non-negotiables

- Preserve the released prologue, RP-001 through RP-012, the RP-012 ending,
  `successor=null`, and all existing adjacency and invariant-world contracts.
- The entire traversal remains possible on foot through stable surfaces,
  incidental shelves, ramps, ridges, apertures, and already-present passages.
- A coupling is physically present and reachable before its lesson is solved.
  Learning may change the Pilot's understanding or the suit's confidence, never
  geometry, access, lighting, weather, machinery, or world state.
- Physical hosts contain no human monitor, kiosk, keyboard, readable glyph,
  face, eye, answer cue, mastery cue, or literal Python display.
- The suit owns detection, translation, readable UI, Python execution,
  assessment, remediation, saves, and readiness language.
- No terminal grants access, permission, identity, reward, authority, external
  action, story credit, or Machine control.
- Terminal 39 and Terminal 40 remain suit/flight-recorder review surfaces at
  the existing ending, not new Machine couplings or post-ending destinations.
- Never open hidden lore. Never invent RP-013, a successor, post-ending content,
  or a physical response from the city.
- Image Specialist, image generation, editing, selection, boards, imports, and
  reveal remain disabled for this pass.

## Quality bar and platform assumptions

The walkthrough must support premium first-person cinematic photorealism while
remaining buildable, accessible, private, offline-first, deterministic,
recoverable, keyboard/mouse/touch operable, reduced-motion safe, and readable
without color-only or timing-only information. Route logic must remain clear
with no protagonist body, hands, shadow, reflection, ship, or human trace.

Every location entry must define: entry orientation, walkable circuit, ordered
host placements, discovery owner, physical fit, adjacency reason, unchanged
exit, and continuity verdict. Drowned Archive must read as one traversable
basin circuit rather than a row of lesson kiosks. Two-cluster city locations
must use local topology and suit diagnosis to motivate the second coupling
without implying a Machine invitation.

## Scope and non-goals

### In scope

- Narrative and spatial continuity for the existing forty lesson clusters.
- Placement correction, renaming, or host refinement where needed for surface
  canon, accessibility, route clarity, or material plausibility.
- Explicit Pilot-first, suit-first, or convergent discovery classification.
- Existing-location entry, inter-host movement, and exit continuity.

### Not in scope

- Runtime construction, scene or curriculum implementation, shell design,
  learning-content changes, new objectives, new packets, or new story beats.
- Image prompts, generation, selection, import, provenance, or cycle reveal.
- Flight between locations, a larger planetary model, human-made trails, or
  claims about Builders, the Machine, city purpose, or terminal intent.

## Priority order

1. Ending, canon, no-authority, and objective integrity.
2. Exact on-foot accessibility and contiguous released adjacency.
3. Causal discovery and movement logic between every cluster.
4. Location-specific material plausibility and visual distinction.
5. Production usefulness for a later, separately authorized image pass.

## Success signals

- All forty clusters are accounted for exactly once with unchanged ownership.
- Every physical host is reachable on foot or explicitly identified as a
  suit-owned nonphysical review surface.
- Every transition answers: why here, who noticed it, and why continue there.
- No transition depends on a solved exercise changing the physical world.
- Every location closes on its already-approved exit and next adjacency.
- The route contains no invented lore, answer-bearing scenery, RP-013,
  successor, post-ending extension, or image work.

## Reopen triggers and protected tradeoffs

Return to Martin before introducing flight, changing location order, combining
or splitting objectives, moving the ending, adding a world response, or making
the Machine an intentional guide. Later production may simplify a host's
ornament, but may not trade away its access logic, discovery classification,
location fit, or the stable suit-interface rule.
