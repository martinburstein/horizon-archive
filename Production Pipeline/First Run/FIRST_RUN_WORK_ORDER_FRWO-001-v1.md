## Player problem and intended outcome

The full released game introduces itself as a frozen demo, exposes temporary
production labels in the prologue, says Drowned success creates route geometry,
makes the fallen Witness assembly track/listen/speak, and interrupts the rail
with obsolete credits claiming the Prologue is complete and that the city
recorded the arrival as a continuation.

The player should instead feel one authored journey: the package is truthful,
the Pilot begins from mortal curiosity rather than a production placeholder,
the suit reports only local evidence, the city never answers, and Chapter IV
follows Chapter III because the physical path was already there.
