capable enough to distinguish foundation, repair, and present service work from

precisely while making fewer unsupported claims.

## Arrival, orientation, attention, tension, mastery, relief, and exit

### Arrival and orientation

The predecessor leaves the player's attention on taut, comparatively small
members, contact wear, wind, and a receded basin edge. The new encounter should
release that close scrutiny into a broad, still inclined face. Dry footing and
the nearby high-water context establish physical safety and continuity before
the face becomes an interactive reading. The player should feel that they have
walked to the next exposed relationship, not arrived at a station placed for
them.

### Attention and tension

Attention widens rather than simply climbing another object ladder. First the
player understands the single broad mass and its dry approach. Then one
irregular deposition relation draws a comparative sweep across visibly
different construction histories. Material boundaries interrupt that sweep;
weathering, joints, erosion, and repair make the distinctions credible without
color doing the explanatory work. Only after the crossings are understood do
the continuing service seams carry attention beyond the face and out of the
present reading.

The tension is epistemic. The provisional name and the repeated band invite a
human urge to read a ledger, chronology, or intentional record. The physical
evidence must answer only with relative crossing and stewardship difference.
Nothing resolves date, author, duration, native purpose, ownership, or intended
audience. Restraint should feel active and satisfying, not like missing content.

### Mastery and relief

The world supplies the comparison; the suit turns the player's attention
inward to objective-by-objective evidence and then to exact weak-area or
readiness-maintenance routing. The learning work may become dense, but it must
not visually or rhetorically transform the mineral relation into a table,
scorecard, answer key, or native curriculum. The player's mastery is the
ability to maintain precise boundaries across many objectives. The world's
stillness is the corroborating counterpoint, never the grader.

Relief arrives when the player can name what the evidence supports and stop
there. On return and completion, the same face, band, waterline, and seams
remain. The absence of ceremony is not bleakness or punishment; it is calm
proof that expedition evidence can become more complete while the basin
continues on its own terms.

### Exit rhythm

The exit is a quiet release from broad comparative reading into the existing
service continuity beyond the encounter. The seams may sustain an onward
direction, but they end outside this bounded physical sentence and do not
depict, activate, illuminate, or confer importance on the later grounded
structure. The next boundary remains separate and unchanged. The local rhythm
adds one unforced observation beat before the existing learning sequence and
no flourish, countdown, or acknowledgement after it.

## Buildable sensory hierarchy

### Visual and material hierarchy

1. Broad dry inclined face and safe same-basin approach establish the field of
   view and player agency.
2. One irregular deposition relation holds the comparative reading together.
3. Foundation, later repair, and present service skin separate through form,
   joints, erosion, layering, and repair vocabulary before palette difference.
4. Crossings between the band and all three histories carry the encounter's
   primary evidence.
5. Several service seams continue beyond the face as subordinate physical
   continuity, not arrows, glyphs, UI rails, or a revealed destination.
6. Receded water and basin atmosphere preserve adjacency without competing for
   the interactive center.

Lighting is patient and physically motivated: broad basin illumination and
restrained reflected light may rake differently across old mass, repair
fabric, present skin, and mineral accretion so their construction reads.

lesson state. Shadow retains material information; darkness, fog, color, and
glare may not conceal missing physical evidence.

The palette should feel mineral, weathered, and continuous with the Drowned
basin while giving this face more earthen mass and less glass-like sparkle than
the predecessor. Color supports separation but never carries history alone.
Calm negative space protects the complete relationship and the suit interface
without turning the face into a backdrop or monument.
