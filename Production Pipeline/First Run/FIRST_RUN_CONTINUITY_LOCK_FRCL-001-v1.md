## Disposition

**`CONTINUITY LOCK / FRCL-001-v1`**

The complete First Run story is coherent, closed, and implementable without
new canon. The released runtime preserves the decisive late-game epistemic arc,
learning ownership, world invariance, and RP-012 hard stop, but it does not yet
express the complete forty-host pedestrian journey. The most serious current
continuity defects are concentrated before City Threshold: visible temporary
prologue scaffolding, a stale mid-rail “Prologue complete” credits interruption,
world-response language, a spoken Witness line, and a compact Drowned Archive
that turns twelve lesson clusters into one stationary Terminal sequence.

This lock classifies that distance; it does not select a Work Order or authorize
implementation.

## Classification method

Each chapter and Terminal receives one primary runtime-parity status:

- `EXACT`: the released player-facing expression preserves the required host or
  chapter identity, ownership, observation, transition, and meaning.
- `ACCEPTED COMPRESSION`: the released expression preserves the complete
  required meaning in a smaller presentation. This does not authorize a false
  claim that an enriched physical host exists.
- `MISSING EXPRESSION`: required canon exists in the game-on-paper but has no
  distinct player-facing runtime expression.
- `CONTRADICTION`: released copy or behavior asserts something the closed canon
  forbids. Contradiction takes precedence over missing detail.
- `UNKNOWN`: inspected authority cannot establish whether the expression is
  present. Unknowns are not promoted to parity.

For a physical Terminal, a complete lesson hidden behind a generic menu is not
host parity. The game promise is one consistent suit interface discovered
through forty physically distinct local surfaces. Learning implementation may
therefore be exact while the host remains `MISSING EXPRESSION`.

## Campaign-wide continuity lock

### Entry knowledge

At the first sanctioned player input, the player may know only that:

1. they are a mortal human Pilot beginning or resuming one local expedition;
2. the setting is an abandoned Builder city whose works continue without their
   makers;
3. a repeating local compatibility signal exists, but no sender, purpose,
   invitation, destination, or judgment is known;
4. the suit can render a human-readable local interface and store sanitized
   expedition evidence;
5. Python is the Pilot's real local interaction method; and
6. the route ahead is investigation, not a mission issued by the Machine.

The opening may establish mortal longing and the Pilot's envy of Builder time.
It may not establish that the Builders envied humans, that the Machine chose the
Pilot, that any signal was sent to the Pilot, or that the city expects a user.

### Emotional escalation

The required first-run arc is:

1. **Projection:** compatibility feels personal; the Pilot wants recurrence to
   imply invitation.
2. **Displacement:** the Witness Corridor proves that resemblance has been
   steering attention; the body-shaped object is not the privileged interface.
3. **Restraint under abundance:** operating systems, closed boundaries,
   convergence, recurrence, and contact remain facts without becoming a
   speaker, permission, or single theory.
4. **Method under pressure:** better tools make overclaiming easier, so the
   Pilot learns to preserve counterexample, ambiguity, and unavailable evidence.
5. **Self-measurement:** the final complete instrument belongs to the suit and
   Pilot, not the world. The Pilot accepts an honest bounded measure and leaves
   the horizon unanswered.

No success, miss, save, restore, confidence choice, timing state, or outcome may
change this order or make the player more or less canonically worthy.
