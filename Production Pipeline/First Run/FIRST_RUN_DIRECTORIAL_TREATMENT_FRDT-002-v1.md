## Disposition

**`DIRECTORIAL LOCK / FRDT-002-v1`**

The shell has one coherent, buildable interpretation over the immutable

## One-sentence felt improvement

Route mastery should send the player's attention not triumphantly onward but
quietly back across ground they already crossed, where the suit recognizes an
ordinary repair relationship as the third place the expedition's own familiar
interface can couple.

## Player fantasy

I am learning to survey an alien civic work with more discipline than I
arrived with: I can follow a material route, let my suit notice what my eyes
dismissed, choose whether to connect, and recognize my own tooling without
pretending that the landscape arranged itself for me.

The fantasy rests on four distinctions:

- Route Marker mastery improves the expedition's reading; it does not activate
  the repair stock or earn the route.
- The Nursery is humble process material, not a prize, shrine, console, cache,
  or secret door.
- `USE` is a deliberate human connection to an already-compatible local
  relationship; the scored work remains optional.
- Three radically unlike bodies exposing one stable interface reveals the
  expedition's consistency, not Builder intent or native Python.

## The felt discovery and attention path

### Before Route Marker mastery - an unnoticed foreground

The accepted plate already places low bulbs, sleeves, broken-looking edges,
collars, channels, and sorted glass across the near-left foreground. They are
visually present during First Signal and Route Marker work, but their low
silhouette and material continuity let them read as process residue rather
than a destination. The player is allowed to have seen them without having
understood them.

Attention initially follows the existing authored hierarchy: immense pale
horizon and cultivated field, dense central First Signal relationship, then
the separate far-right Route Marker. The low-left stock stays quiet. There is
no hotspot, label, pulse, sound, contrast change, false locked state, or
suggestion that it is waiting.

### Route Marker mastery - the counterturn

The Route Marker's inherited physical response remains the sole world-side
progression exception. Once its overlay closes, that restrained right-edge
geometry still owns the first glance and the Drowned departure remains plainly
available. Then one polite suit-owned compatibility notice redirects attention
across the unchanged foreground toward the low-left stock.

This is a short retracing beat, not a new expedition leg. Screen space makes it
feel like looking back over the few steps just crossed: the Marker is farther
right and more formally legible; the Nursery is nearer, lower, irregular, and
easy to have passed. The reversal is the emotional point. Better method has
made the overlooked visible without making it new.

The plate must not animate, brighten, heal, open, or change crop. Discovery is
carried by semantic availability, restrained hierarchy, and the player's own
reorientation. Tactical may determine the exact code-native cue and responsive
label placement, but the cue must remain secondary to the unchanged world and
must never conceal the always-open departure.

### At the Nursery - humble work, deliberate connection

The low-left grouping should read as one repair-return relationship rather
than a pile of props: rejected cloudy forms, imperfect sleeves, fused edges,
low collars, and nearby feed/return channels occupy the same shallow process
zone. Its scale is intimate only by comparison with the horizon. It is not
made monumental to prove importance.

`LOOK AT` belongs to material observation. `TALK TO` lands on complete silence.
Neither beat should feel like trying the wrong password. The pause after
silence matters: no voice, hum, chime, motion, or status reaction answers the
player. Deliberate `USE` then hands visual and semantic authority to the same
human expedition Terminal interface already learned elsewhere. No second
campaign launcher competes with that physical connection.

### After optional practice - recognition, not reward

Whether the player skips, exits, misses, remediates, resumes, or masters the
existing calibration, the same field returns. The cracks stay cracked, the
Marker stays completed, and the Drowned route stays open. A mastered return may
feel settled through concise expedition evidence and stable hierarchy, never
through a world reaction.

The chapter turn lands when the player mentally compares the three encounters:
the dense central coupling, the precise far-right Marker, and the low-left
repair stock are physically dissimilar, yet the overlay has been consistent.
That consistency now reads as the expedition's interface following the Pilot
through the world. The final move is a short look back to the already-readable
rightward relationship and a deliberate departure over the same ridge.

## Pacing and silence lock

The changed beat should be brief outside the optional lesson:

1. Route Terminal closes; allow the completed Marker and open route one calm
   visual beat.
2. One polite suit-owned detection redirects attention to the low-left stock.
3. The player may immediately depart or take the short visual counterturn.
4. Observation is concise; silence after `TALK TO` is complete and unteased.
5. `USE` opens the existing calibration without a flourish or extra mandatory
   confirmation.
6. Every close returns the unchanged plate and keeps departure legible.
7. The compact three-body comparison precedes departure without becoming a
   blocking cinematic or exposition card.

No timer, camera pan, forced focus animation, automatic Terminal opening,
sound cue, success sting, ambient voice, or new media sets this pace. Resume
and Drowned-return states enter at their current truth; they do not replay
discovery as if the world reacted again.

## World, interface, and lesson relationship

The pass should make the causal boundary unusually clear:

1. **World:** repair stock has always occupied the low-left foreground.
2. **Route evidence:** existing Route Marker mastery lets the expedition
   distinguish one more compatible relationship; it does not alter matter.
3. **Suit:** a local classification makes the relationship legible without
   assigning Builder name, function, audience, or intent.
4. **Pilot:** the player chooses whether to approach and whether to `USE`.
5. **Human interface:** the expedition overlay owns Python, prompts, files,
   errors, remediation, confidence, evidence, privacy, and resume behavior.
6. **World returned:** the same stock, cracks, field, Marker, and ridge remain.
7. **Interpretation:** three bodies and one familiar overlay reveal human
   interface continuity; Machine and Builders still say nothing.

The lesson's mistakes belong to a human working copy, never to the object.
The optional calibration can refine the player's method but cannot earn,
repair, open, illuminate, or authorize any part of the landscape.

## Adjacent-scene contrast

The Meadow should close on a small, bright, horizontal act of reconsideration;
the Drowned Archive then opens into cool, vertical, water-dark immensity.

The Nursery is low, near, dry, intricate, and made of retained imperfection.
The Drowned arrival is vast, wet, remote, and dominated by a grounded local
coupling beneath a distant suspended landmark. The transition therefore moves
from intimate repair stock to overwhelming phase infrastructure without a
portal, vehicle, flourish, or reward shot.

Do not let the Nursery borrow the Drowned Archive's mystery-landmark grammar.
It should be less spectacular than First Signal and less directive than the
Route Marker. Its chill comes from modesty: the place where the player learns
the most important interface distinction was physically present at their feet
all along.
