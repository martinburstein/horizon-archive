## One-sentence felt improvement

The first run should feel like one continuous descent from private human longing
into earned interpretive restraint: each local success clarifies the Pilot's own
method, then gives the unchanged world back, until the vast already-operating
City Threshold arrives as a fact rather than a reward.

## Player fantasy

I am a mortal field investigator whose suit can recognize usable local
couplings in an impossible abandoned city. I do not command the city, and the
city does not grade me. I choose to connect, learn real method, correct my own
projections, and keep walking because the physical evidence continues.

The fantasy depends on a productive split:

- the world is monumental, old, physically credible, and indifferent;
- the suit is precise, local, human-readable, and recoverable;
- the lesson changes what I can do and notice, not what the city chooses to do;
- the Pilot supplies desire and interpretation, then learns to revise both; and
- forward movement is a human decision along a pre-existing material line.

## Emotional and attentional progression

### 1. Package and title - confidence before mystery

The package should feel finished before the fiction begins. Its identity is
plain, truthful, and quiet: this is Horizon Archive, a complete local game, not
a development artifact. Once the title opens, attention contracts around one
invitation to begin or resume. The title promises a patient mystery, not a
spectacle or an exam outcome.

The emotional note is controlled anticipation. Product instructions and local
save truth should be legible without competing with the title's gravity.

### 2. Three-beat opening - longing, caution, scale

The existing three beats form a deliberate inward sequence:

1. **Pattern:** curiosity acquires a personal edge, but no sender or invitation.
2. **Approach:** mortal urgency meets a reversible choice; the Pilot can still
   turn back.
3. **Horizon:** the scale becomes undeniable while purpose remains unassigned.

Each beat should be brief enough to read in one calm breath. Negative space and
the absence of a world image make these thoughts feel like flight-recorder
entries, not an exposition crawl. The final activation should release the
stored tension directly into the Chapter I landscape, with no production label,
teaser cadence, or extra mandatory step.

### 3. Glass Meadow - release into wonder

The Chapter I reveal is the first expansion of visual attention. The accepted
Meadow plate should move from a subdued chapter card to its full pale-gold,
high-horizon breadth in play. The eye first receives scale and cultivated glass,
then the suit's restrained compatibility cue makes the integrated foreground
coupling legible.

First Signal establishes that connecting is the Pilot's choice. Route Marker
then turns improved recognition into a more precise reading of an already
present relationship. Closing an overlay should restore the same sunlit field,
not a newly rewarded version of it. Fracture Nursery remains absent and must not
be implied by copy, hotspot count, or presentation.

The emotional change is wonder becoming tentative competence. Familiarity is
in the suit interface, not proof that the field expected a human user.

### 4. Drowned Archive - abundance without invitation

The accepted basin plate creates the pass's longest sustained hold: cool water,
enormous process architecture, a local grounded coupling in the near field, and
the Crown maintaining visual dominance at distance. Attention should repeatedly
return from the consistent lesson overlay to those unchanged relationships.

Because the compact runtime owns many lesson clusters at one host, rhythm must
come from the existing work-and-return cycle rather than fictionalizing new
Terminals. Natural completion, remediation, and resume seams may each provide a
short visual or textual reorientation to the basin before the next existing
lesson action becomes primary. No new walk, host, or mandatory activation is
implied. The Crown never brightens, shifts, acknowledges progress, or becomes a
score display.

The emotional change is competence becoming dangerous abundance: many useful
connections can feel like a trail, but the player is learning that availability
does not establish intent. The departure relation is the dry outflow already
present behind the grounded structure.

### 5. Witness Corridor - projection corrected in silence

The accepted Corridor plate compresses the visual field after the open basin.
Its strongest immediate resemblance is the fallen assembly on the right; its
actual usable coupling is the quieter service-laminate body on the left. The
composition should permit the body-shaped misreading while the suit's semantic
hierarchy patiently identifies the separate active surface.

The fallen assembly remains inspectable but inert. Its physical prominence must
not translate into action priority, voice, attention, rejection, or hidden
status. The Evidence Terminal alone opens the familiar overlay. The supplied
evidence audio remains an explicitly player-controlled lesson source inside
that overlay, never ambient speech from the corridor or assembly.

After mastery, the most important beat is silence: local evidence is complete,
the assembly is unchanged, and the walking volume behind the coupling remains
open. The emotional change is not triumph but a small, memorable embarrassment
that sharpens method: resemblance had been directing attention before evidence.

### 6. Direct City Threshold - scale without welcome

The Witness continuation must land directly on the accepted City Threshold
overview. There is no credits card, title reprise, celebratory wipe, spoken
confirmation, or staged unlock between them. The contrast supplies the chill:
the Corridor's close black-and-amber passage gives way to a vast geothermal
vault whose bridges, vapor, light, and maintenance cycles were already active.

The world image should dominate the first City landing while one neutral local
status gives the player orientation before the first required action. Existing
cycle motion may continue at its ordinary rhythm from the first frame; it must
not begin, brighten, accelerate, or synchronize with Witness completion. The
warm amber depth reads as ongoing operation, not celebration. The player gets a
new scale of evidence and no answer.

This completes the pass's emotional sentence: the city is alive with process,
but nothing in it has noticed the player.

## Pacing lock

- Package guidance is functional and short; title dwell remains player-owned.
- The three opening beats are concise, individually resumable, and advanced by
  the existing one-hit activation contract. No timer or forced motion sets pace.
- Chapter I has the quickest discovery-to-first-success cadence and the
  brightest visual release.
- Chapter II is intentionally the longest hold. Its many existing lessons need
  repeated moments of world reorientation, not fabricated host variety.
- Chapter III is comparatively brief and compressed. Its correction should not
  be buried under extra explanation after the long basin sequence.
- Direct City entry is immediate after the earned continuation. Orientation may
  be achieved by hierarchy and focus, never by a blocking cinematic delay.
- Resume states enter at the same emotional truth as fresh states. They do not
  replay arrival, wonder, response, success, or credits beats.

## World, interface, and lesson relationship

The world and interface should remain visibly different systems with a clean
causal boundary.

1. **World first:** each scene establishes a physical relation that exists
   independently of the player.
2. **Suit detection:** a restrained local cue identifies compatibility without
   assigning purpose, ownership, or audience.
3. **Pilot choice:** the player activates the usable surface.
4. **Human overlay:** the same expedition interface takes visual and semantic
   ownership of instruction, code, feedback, remediation, and evidence.
5. **Local result:** success or a miss changes only the overlay, suit-owned
   evidence, and available player action permitted by the shell.
6. **World returned:** closing or completing the overlay reveals the same
   physical scene. The next relation was already present.

No environmental color, light, motion, sound, silence, scale change, or crop
change may encode correctness, worthiness, authority, reward, damage, or world
response. READY and NOT YET READY are outside this pass but remain the governing
model: evidence outcomes belong to the expedition, not the city.

## Adjacent-scene contrast

The pass should read as a controlled sequence of spatial breaths:

`dark interior thought -> bright open field -> cool immense basin -> dark close
corridor -> warm immense city vault`

This is not a simple escalation from darkness to reward. Meadow opens wonder;
Drowned overwhelms with abundance; Witness constricts attention and corrects
projection; City opens scale again while withholding welcome. Chapter IV must
therefore be busier in visible operation but quieter in interpretation than the
Corridor. The player sees more happening and is licensed to conclude less.
