## One-sentence felt improvement

The player arrives at one quiet, reachable construction relationship whose
three overlapping material histories and one indifferent mineral trace become
legible through patient looking, rewarding disciplined observation without

## Player fantasy and emotional contract

The player is a field investigator learning to distinguish process from
appearance. The pleasure comes from seeing how a foundation, a repair, and a
service skin physically meet, then noticing that the same environmental trace
behaves differently on each substrate. The player is not decoding a record or
receiving an answer. They are becoming better at saying exactly what the
evidence supports and leaving authorship, dates, purpose, and intent unknown.

The emotional movement is:

1. **Arrival — grounded safety:** a continuous dry approach and low reachable
   face make the next observation feel like part of the existing walk, not a
   destination erected for the player.
2. **Orientation — one relationship before detail:** the broad concave mass,
   dry foreground, and laterally receded water establish where the player is
   before any material distinction asks for attention.
3. **Inquiry — difference becomes causal:** joints, depth, edges, bridging,
   lamination, and weathering reveal three construction processes that overlap
   rather than forming labeled bands.
4. **Tension — the urge to overread:** the continuous trace and service voids
   suggest history and continuity while refusing message, chronology, purpose,
   or response.
5. **Mastery — comparison with restraint:** the player can follow one trace
   across all three substrates and name three different physical reactions
   without converting the relationship into an answer key.
6. **Relief and exit — world unchanged:** expedition evidence can advance,
   but the face, water, trace, seams, light, and route remain indifferent. The
   existing journey continues without activation or ceremony.

## Directorial composition lock

### Camera and viewpoint

The frozen structural guide is the positional authority. Rendering must feel
like a standing first-person field observation from the dry route, looking
obliquely down and across the face rather than straight at a facade.

- Use a natural, non-telephoto field of view: approximately a `28–35 mm`
  full-frame-equivalent reading, with no extreme wide-angle stretch.
- Eye height reads as ordinary standing height, approximately `1.55–1.75 m`
  above the dry approach; the camera is not elevated, hovering, crouched, or
  placed on the structure.
- Downward pitch should read as roughly `12–20 degrees`; cross-face yaw should
  read as roughly `25–40 degrees`. These are perceptual limits, not authority
  to move frozen guide geometry.
- Keep a level, quiet horizon. No Dutch angle, heroic low angle, axial symmetry,
  straight-on elevation, overhead map view, or monumental reveal.
- The player must be able to infer that the foreground continues physically
  into the observed dry adjacency. The view may not imply a drop, moat,
  impoundment, forbidden threshold, or dedicated observation platform.

The camera must preserve the guide's centered relation, complete dry
foreground, lateral receded-water context, interaction center, label/UI
corridor, and protected margins. Lens drama is subordinate to physical truth.

### Silhouette and mass

The primary silhouette is a **low, broad, concave accreted shelf**, not a wall.
Its upper contour should be broken, shallow, and subordinate to the basin
horizon. Its apparent height must remain reachable and field-scaled; its width
and curvature carry more visual weight than elevation.

The silhouette fails if it suggests a dam, retaining barrier, levee, weir,
spillway, monument, building, platform, gate, cliff, or creature. There is no
crest, parapet, abutment, buttress, tower, portal, access deck, or water held
behind the mass. Receded water remains lateral context, never the load that the
face appears to retain.

Negative space is part of the silhouette. Concavity, broken cells, undercut
edges, recessed capillary voids, and quiet peripheral basin floor prevent the
mass from reading as a planar engineered facade. That negative space must be
materially fused into the shelf rather than attached as hardware.

### Exact attention hierarchy

The player should read the scene in this order:

1. **Dry approach plus low concave whole** — one safe, reachable relationship.
2. **Three construction processes as physical systems** — each distinguished
   through geometry, load behavior, joints, depth, edges, and weathering.
3. **Pairwise interdigitation** — the boundaries repeatedly finger, bridge,
   island, overlap, and interrupt one another horizontally and vertically.
4. **One continuous wandering mineral trace** — a single relation across the
   whole, never three colored stripes.
5. **Three substrate reactions** — collection in foundation cells, feathering
   across repair sinter, and thinning/interruption over service lamellae.
6. **Branching recessed service voids** — subordinate upward continuity that
   leaves the frame without pointing to a destination.
7. **Lateral water and basin atmosphere** — context, not spectacle.
8. **Expedition interface affordance** — actionable and accessible, but never
   visually mistaken for a native label or property of the world.

The first six readings are conjunctive. A beautiful atmosphere cannot replace
one. The trace must not become the first and only legible feature, and the
interface must not dominate the physical relationship.

## Material-process readability lock

The three histories are construction processes, not palette regions.

### Older foundation process

Read as a fused vesicular load mesh: irregular load-spreading ribs, eroded
cells, broken depth, and continuous mineral-like fabrication. It should carry
mass through a distributed internal structure. It must not resemble masonry,
aggregate concrete, blocks, courses, cast panels, or ordinary porous rock.

### Later repair process

Read as matte ceramic sinter that repairs specific failures through unequal
dendritic bridging fingers, fused collars, and irregular islands. Its logic is
localized attachment and load transfer across older breaks. It must not become
rectangular patchwork, plaster, grout, mortar, panel replacement, a clean band,
or a cosmetic color overlay.

### Present service-skin process

Read as a thin discontinuous lamellar skin bonded over portions of both older
processes. Curl, delamination, exposed edges, changing thickness, and relief-
following contact establish its assembly logic. It must not become cladding
panels, sheet metal, roofing, a continuous cap, scales, bark, membrane biology,
or decorative trim.

### Interdigitation law

Every material pair must visibly interlock at least twice, and the
interlocks must survive as three-dimensional construction evidence. Fingers,
islands, collars, overlaps, undercuts, and delaminated edges should cross both
horizontal and vertical ranges. No process may occupy a simple top, middle, or
bottom zone. No boundary may read as a straight seam separating three colored
layers.

Interdigitation has the highest material priority: if texture richness competes
with readable overlap, simplify texture. If color separation competes with
joint/depth evidence, reduce color contrast and strengthen the physical join.
If an edit improves one process by erasing a passing pairwise boundary, it is a
regression, not a repair.

## Trace and substrate-reaction lock

There is one broken, wandering, mineral high-water crust. It is continuous as
a physical history even where local coverage thins or disappears briefly. Its
path is irregular and topographically responsive; it must not be ruler-straight,
evenly thick, repeated, highlighted, or horizontally organized like a timeline.

Its three reactions are locally distinct but recognizably part of the same
deposit:

- in the vesicular foundation, material collects within cells and behind rib
  edges, producing thicker pockets and protected residue;
- across ceramic repair, it feathers and diffuses over matte fused surfaces,
  with soft broken margins; and
- over the lamellar skin, it thins, skips, and catches at delaminated edges,
  showing weak adhesion without becoming a separate stripe.

The trace is not writing, a graph, a route, a status indicator, a chronology,
an authored ledger, an answer, or a signal. It never glows, animates, points,
aligns with interface rows, changes after interaction, or becomes more complete
when the player succeeds.

## Service-void and negative-space lock

The upward continuities are **recessed capillary voids**, not attached service
objects. At least three distinct paths climb through the face and leave the
frame. They are unequal in width and spacing, nonparallel, and locally absorbed
by each material process. At least one clearly branches or merges.

Their visual grammar is absence: narrow shadow, undercut depth, eroded opening,
and fused material edge. No circular section, manufactured bore, cover, flange,
clamp, fastener, grille, cable, pipe, conduit, drain, rail, ladder, channel,
panel joint, repeating module, or lit interior is permitted. They contain no
fluid, pulse, directional motion, glyph, destination, invitation, or response.

Void depth should be legible through soft occlusion and material-edge behavior,
not black graphic lines. The group remains subordinate to the trace and
process boundaries. It preserves the sense that service continuity extends
beyond this reading without revealing or emphasizing the later boundary.

## Lighting, color, atmosphere, audio, and motion

### Lighting

Use diffuse overcast basin light with restrained cross-raking illumination.
The principal light should reveal relief through broad edge gradients, shallow
occlusion, joint depth, delamination, and weathering—not through spotlighting.
The foundation reads through cell depth and rib shadow; the repair through
matte bridging and fused collars; the skin through thin edges and soft
specular breakup.

Do not use emission, bloom, rim-glow, colored activation, scanning light,
dramatic god rays, lesson-state changes, or a high-contrast destination beam.
Deep voids remain readable as depth, not pure graphic black. Shadows cannot
hide missing interlocks, reactions, route continuity, or protected violations.

### Color and material separation

Use one restrained basin family—weathered mineral neutrals, muted ceramic
earths, and oxidized low-saturation skin tones. Separate processes primarily
by relief, edge, bond, thickness, joint, reflectance, and weathering behavior.
Hue separation is secondary and must remain understandable in desaturation and
forced-color presentation.

Never reproduce the guide palette or make the three processes a brown/orange/
blue key. Avoid fluorescent accents, saturated control colors, metallic sci-fi
trim, pristine surfaces, symbolic crystals, or value patterns resembling UI.
The trace may be slightly lighter through deposition but must remain mineral,
broken, and subordinate to the whole.

### Audio, motion, and silence

No new audio or motion is requested. Existing basin wind and distant water may
continue at an indifferent, unsynchronized level. They may not trace the
mineral line, identify a process, cue USE, mark correctness, swell at mastery,
suggest speech, or imply that the world noticed the player.

The face itself is still. No seam pulses, skin breathes, repair grows, mineral
trace moves, water responds, panel opens, or light changes on LOOK, TALK, USE,
failure, remediation, completion, reload, or return. Silence is observational
room, not a pause waiting for an answer.
