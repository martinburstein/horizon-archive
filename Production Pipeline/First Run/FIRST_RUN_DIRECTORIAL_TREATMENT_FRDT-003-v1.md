## Player fantasy

I am walking a basin whose relationships were not arranged for a human body,
yet I can keep my footing, notice repetition without mistaking it for an
answer, and choose one careful local connection while the vast process around
me continues without acknowledging my work.

The fantasy depends on five distinctions:

- Host 04 teaches the expedition how to sort a local evidence problem; it does
  not activate, point at, or send the player to Host 05.
- The Pilot notices the repeated six-part relationship before the suit confirms
  compatibility. The human name `Sixfold Weir` remains a useful analogy.
- Host 05 is a distributed relation across two dry mineral lips, not a screen,
  kiosk, control panel, diagram, or six-principle mnemonic.
- The stable interface belongs to the expedition. Responsible-AI categories,
  feedback, remediation, confidence, and evidence never belong to the water or
  branches.
- Mastery changes the Pilot's evidence and next choice; the basin, branches,
  Crown, water, and route do not respond.

## Causal walk and attention path

### Before Host 04 mastery - visible process, absent interface

The accepted plate already shows the foreground relationship. Host 04 occupies
the left-of-center ridge/water junction; Host 05 lies lower and farther right,
where several pale-blue and weathered metal relations cross the foreground
across two dry lips. The eye may register those lines as part of the basin's
larger processing fabric, but there is no Host 05 DOM node, focus stop, stable
name, state label, invitation, pulse, lock, or credit.

The scenic scan remains broad: reflective basin, repeated monumental supports,
distant circular landmark, grounded Host 04, then the irregular near-field
fabric. The Tidal Lens may dominate the horizon but must never become the
Host 05 target or the implied destination of the work.

### Host 04 closes - a downward clockwise reorientation

When exact Workload Sort mastery closes the Host 04 Terminal, the player first
gets the unchanged basin back. The next attention move is not a forward unlock
or a celebratory sweep. It is a short clockwise/downward reconsideration of
the same dry foreground: follow the visible relation leaving the grounded
coupling toward six unequal branches that cross the adjacent mineral lips.

This is the causal answer to why Host 04 leads to Host 05. Workload Sort gives
the Pilot a better way to notice repeated evidence needs; the world supplies a
physically adjacent repeated relation that was already visible. The Coupler
does not point, signal, open, illuminate, or change. The Pilot makes the first
interpretive move; one polite suit-owned compatibility confirmation follows.

At wide layouts the move should read as a few dry steps across one continuous
foreground, from the compact Host 04 body toward a lower, spread-out
relationship. At narrow and retained `320` layouts, the exact adjacent targets
and semantic hierarchy must carry the same turn without a required camera pan,
zoom, timed animation, or loss of the wider basin. The one-percent narrow gap
is enough to keep the bodies distinct, but the label and focus treatment must
not visually fuse them.

### Approach - two lips, no crossing

Both mineral lips are reachable from the same stable ridge. The player does
not wade, swim, jump a live channel, climb the landmark, step onto submerged
machinery, or use a human-designed walkway. The approach is a short incidental
dry passage: one lip presents part of the relation at one orientation, and the
second carries the remaining branches at another. No Builder-scale feature
agrees on a human operating height, which is precisely why the host should feel
distributed rather than console-like.

The physical read must remain asymmetric. Six relations share one inflow but
do not become six equal lanes, six labeled arms, six lights, or a tidy radial
diagram. The visual rhyme motivates inspection; it supplies no responsible-AI
answer and maps to no principle.

### At the Weir - observation, silence, deliberate connection

`LOOK AT` belongs first to the visible material fact: unequal branch relations,
two dry lips, one inflow, and the reachable local coupling. The Pilot may give
the relationship its provisional human name. The suit may confirm only a
separate compatible local surface and current expedition state.

`TALK TO` ends in complete silence. Water and the plate remain exactly as they
were. No delayed response, broken speaker, dormant intelligence, recognition,
rejection, future promise, or hidden message follows.

Only deliberate `USE Sixfold Weir` opens or reconstructs the existing
`L02-02` expedition Terminal. This is the sole in-world campaign entry, not an
automatic continuation and not a duplicated launcher in the command panel.
The interface may be visually familiar; the host body must remain radically
unlike the compact Tide-Sort coupling and the Meadow repair stock.

### Work, miss, return, and mastery - the water never grades

During responsible-AI work, all correctness, remediation, confidence, and
ownership language remains inside the human overlay. A miss narrows the actual
dimension and nowhere else. Closing, Escape, primary acknowledgement, reload,
and Meadow return/redeparture restore the same physical relation without
replaying discovery as a world event.

An unfinished return should feel like coming back to a marked place in one's
own notes: the suit can truthfully report that expedition work may resume, but
the branches show no unfinished condition. A mastered return should feel even
quieter. The system can report finalized existing evidence; `USE` is read-only;
the basin offers no approval, repair, change, or reaction.

After mastery, attention can follow the pre-existing material continuation
toward lens-like trained fragments and the current `L02-03` action. This pass
does not name, visualize, register, or promise Host 06. The player moves because
the material trail was already there, not because Sixfold Weir rewards them.
