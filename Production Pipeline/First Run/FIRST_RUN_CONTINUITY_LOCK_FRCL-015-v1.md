## Material-ID and guide semantics

Guide colors, numeric IDs, layer names, masks, control edges, construction
lines, labels, legends, metadata, bounding boxes, and anchor marks are
production-only control signals. They have no in-world color, language,
taxonomy, authorship, chronology, value, or authority. They must be absent
from the final player-facing source and may not be treated as proof that the
physical meaning survived rendering.

Material IDs may distinguish process families and local reactions, but they
may not encode:

- written language, pseudo-writing, glyphs, dates, numerals, scores, objective
  rows, lesson answers, Azure/Microsoft marks, or a native ledger;
- names, identities, owners, builders, eras, absolute chronology, purpose,
  intent, judgment, invitation, access, reward, or completion;
- hidden-lore answers, a continuity mechanism, consciousness, Machine
  category, Builder disappearance, or the Crown's nature;
- world-state changes, response states, pass/fail states, route unlocks, save
  fields, readiness outcomes, or successor state; or
- human ergonomics, civil infrastructure, tools, bodies, vehicles, signage,
  ritual furniture, familiar control hardware, or prior-human trace.

The material guide must remain meaningfully editable: topology, process
regions, crossings, reactions, seams, negative space, and protected areas need
independent control. It must also remain independently falsifiable: a final
render cannot pass because its hidden guide or mask was correct when the
player-facing physical evidence is absent.
