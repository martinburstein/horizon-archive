## New causal representation mechanism

Replace the barrier-and-conduit grammar with a **low concave accreted basin
shelf whose three construction processes interdigitate and whose service
continuity exists as branching negative space**.

### Composition law: shelf, not barrier

- View obliquely down and across a low, broad, concave exposed basin shelf from
  standing height. The dry foreground remains physically continuous into the
  inclined face.
- The face is part of the basin floor/shelf, not a wall retaining water. It has
  no crest, abutment, spillway, parapet, towering facade, vertical concrete
  mass, or water impounded behind it.
- Receded same-basin water appears laterally beyond the dry shelf or in a
  shallow distant depression. The waterline trace wraps across the exposed
  topography; it does not terminate against a dam-like wall.
- Keep the complete dry approach, face, three histories, one trace, at least
  three service seams, and water context inside the existing centered crop-safe
  core. Quiet peripheral basin floor, not monumental skyline, supplies margin.

### Material-process law: interdigitation, not strata

All three histories occupy overlapping vertical and horizontal ranges. None may
be a top, middle, or bottom strip, and none may be merely a different color.

1. **Ancient foundation fabric:** a fused vesicular mineral mesh with irregular
   load-spreading ribs, eroded cells, and broken depth. It is continuous rocklike
   construction, not masonry, poured concrete, blocks, panels, or aggregate.
2. **Later repair fabric:** localized matte ceramic sinter that bridges old
   fractures with unequal dendritic fingers and fused collars. It crosses into
   the foundation in irregular islands rather than forming rectangular patches,
   courses, bands, or panel joints.
3. **Present service skin:** a thin discontinuous lamellar membrane bonded over
   portions of both older fabrics, curling, delaminating, and exposing its edge.
   It follows local relief rather than forming cladding panels or a horizontal
   cap.

At least two visible interlocking fingers or islands must interrupt each
material boundary. Each history must extend both above and below part of at
least one other history so private review can reject simple horizontal zoning.

### Crossing law: one natural trace, three substrate reactions

One broken, wandering mineral high-water crust crosses all three independent
histories as a continuous topographic relation. It may thin across the lamellar
skin, collect inside vesicular foundation cells, and feather over the sintered
repair, but those local reactions must visibly belong to the same irregular
trace. It cannot become three separate stripes, an orderly row, a timeline, a
glyph, or a lesson diagram.

### Service law: branching negative space, not hardware

At least three distinct pre-existing service seams climb away from the crossed
face and leave the frame. Represent them as narrow recessed fissure networks or
shadowed capillary voids fused into the material processes:

- unequal width, unequal spacing, and nonparallel paths;
- at least one branch or merge visible across the group;
- no circular pipe section, tube, conduit, cable, clamp, bracket, bolt,
  fastener, drain hole, grate, channel cover, expansion joint, or repeated
  construction module;
- no glow, fluid, motion, activation, arrow, convergence, destination reveal,
  or world response.

The seams preserve physical service continuity while denying familiar human
hardware and without implying biology, writing, or sentience.
