## Player problem and intended felt outcome

Today, Route Marker mastery immediately presents a generic dialogue choice for
`Optional calibration`. The strict `L01-03` lesson exists and persists safely,
but it has no distinct physical host. A new player therefore encounters two
Meadow bodies, not the locked three, and misses the authored realization that
the stable familiar interface belongs to the human expedition rather than the
alien field.

After this pass, the player should feel that the suit noticed something they
would have walked past: low repair stock reveals a third compatible coupling,
physically unlike the other two. Connecting still opens the same consistent
human expedition interface. Whether the player completes the optional practice
or departs, the route stays open, the cracks and field remain unchanged, and
the chapter closes on recognition rather than reward.
