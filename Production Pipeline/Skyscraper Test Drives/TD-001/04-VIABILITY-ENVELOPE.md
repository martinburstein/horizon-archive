Every intent must carry the exact shell/packet/controller version, campaign mode, expected owner, exact current phase, one approved action, one of the seven approved modalities, an opaque fresh event token, and no private payload outside the named learner fields.

Validation occurs before token consumption.

- Invalid, stale, duplicate, combined, wrong-phase, wrong-owner, Tour, private-bearing, or later-state intent is rejected without consuming a future valid token.
- A fresh valid token can produce at most one state replacement and one finalized evidence write.
- Visibility, focus, hover, timing, key repeat, replay, restored intent, or disabled-control activation produces no transition or evidence.
- A token is never persisted and cannot be replayed after resume.
- A failed storage/checkpoint commit leaves the last exact finalized checkpoint unchanged, clears private/transient work, and reconstructs its blank first-incomplete form.
