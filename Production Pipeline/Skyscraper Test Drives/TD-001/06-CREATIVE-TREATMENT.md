## One-sentence emotional promise

> Hold one precise human condition against a civilization-scale working mystery, prove that the method is yours, and leave more capable while the sealed greater whole remains exactly as unresolved as before.

## Player fantasy

The player is a careful field analyst inside a mortal pilot's suit, working at the edge of comprehension rather than at the center of a system designed for them.

The fantasy is not “make the alien machine obey.” It is:

- choose to inspect rather than be summoned;
- build a trustworthy local method from exposed evidence;
- recover from a miss without punishment or an answer key;
- close the earlier work and reconstruct the boundary from memory;
- apply the same reasoning independently in a fresh context; and
- recognize that increased human capability does not make the world smaller, friendlier, or more legible than the evidence permits.

The emotional reward belongs entirely to the player's developing discipline.

## Creative thesis: the quiet working margin

`SS-RP003-PY010-v1` should feel like doing exact, private fieldwork beside an immense process that neither pauses nor acknowledges the work.

The dominant contrast is:

```text
world: continuous, old, immense, physically indifferent
expedition layer: temporary, exact, human-owned, increasingly self-sufficient
```

The SC-04 world is never a backdrop to be “won.” Its stable practical light, repeating structure, sealed boundary, and ongoing environmental clock keep pressing a larger scale into the player's peripheral awareness. The expedition layer should feel intentionally removable: a disciplined work surface held in suit space, clearly separate from Builder matter and never aligned to resemble a native screen, lock, face, or reply.

## Attention hierarchy

Every state preserves one active owner/message/content/action group. Within that group, attention should resolve in this order:

1. **The active owner and complete stage heading** — immediately answers “whose layer is this and what work is current?”
2. **The one present learner responsibility** — blank fields, retrieval boundary, repair acknowledgement, or no-action final status.
3. **The single next valid action or recovery route** — explicit, semantic, and never communicated by color, sound, motion, or placement alone.
4. **Local status and answer-free guidance** — close to the relevant control and subordinate to the player's work.
5. **The invariant SC-04 greater whole** — visually dominant at the shell level, continuously present as context, never converted into feedback.
6. **Secondary safe return** — discoverable and honest but unable to compete with the current primary responsibility.

Wide composition may let the world and work layer remain simultaneously legible. Narrow and effective-`200%` layouts must use natural source order: world first, then owner, heading, current content, status, primary action, and safe return. No required action may sit over world art.

## Pacing profile

The rhythm is deliberate rather than cinematic-spectacular:

1. **Threshold — one breath:** the fresh review activation is immediate and unadorned.
2. **Primary — sustained concentration:** this is the longest authored beat and may not be rushed by a timer, animated urgency, or progressive answer revelation.
3. **Repair — short and exact:** an actual miss creates a compact diagnostic pause, one explicit acknowledgement, complete clearing, and a clean retry. Repeated misses remain equally calm.
4. **Retrieval — compressed recollection:** the four dimensions arrive as one coherent closed-note responsibility, not four dramatic reveals.
5. **Transfer — clean expansion:** the new unseen case gets the clearest empty working field and the least explanatory framing.
6. **Landing — held silence:** finalization replaces the task with one concise no-action status. The world gets no sting, pulse, relight, opening, camera beat, or route cue.

State replacement should feel decisive. Decorative waiting, countdowns, score-counting animations, confetti, “success” sweeps, failure shakes, and delayed permission are out of character.

## World and expedition-interface relationship

### World

- SC-04 remains a civilization-scale working cross-section, not a classroom, laboratory, console bay, shrine, factory floor, or human workstation.
- The registered world presentation remains stable across entry, work, miss, retry, retrieval, transfer, and landing.
- Environmental operation may continue only through already-authorized, monotonic ambient behavior. It never synchronizes with input, evaluation, checkpoint, or finalization.
- The ordinary bounded difference receives no privileged light, color, crop, sound, focus, animation, or compositional elevation.
- The fused sealed boundary remains non-affordant. It is never framed as a disabled control, denied route, answer source, reward chamber, or tempting next action.

### Expedition layer

- The work surface reads as a **temporary field folio in suit space**: matte, exact, removable, and unmistakably human/expedition-owned.
- It does not visually “grow out of” the coupling or conform to Builder geometry. The coupling establishes physical adjacency only; the separate interface owns all readable Python and feedback.
- Owner labels do structural work. `PILOT`, `SYSTEM`, `TEACHER`, and `BUILDER WORK` cannot merge into one decorative voice.
- One active group fully replaces another. Prior code, completed output, feedback, and controls are absent rather than merely dimmed behind the next task.
- Blankness is generous negative space, a visible condition of authorship, not a disabled or unfinished application state.

- Add no runtime audio asset.

- There is no correctness sting, failure alarm, reward chord, sealed-boundary sound, Machine voice, Builder voice, or world acknowledgement.

### Motion

- The world retains only already-authorized monotonic ambient motion; it never restarts, accelerates, synchronizes, brightens, or pauses for player work.
- Interface transitions should be restrained direct replacements. If existing CSS motion is reused, it may clarify group replacement but may not delay focus, encode outcome, or exceed what reduced-motion mode can replace instantly.
- Misses do not shake, flash, recoil, distort, or darken the world or field folio.
- The `PY010-P3` landing is stillness with ordinary environmental continuation, not a hero shot.
