## One-sentence emotional promise

> Build an honest human record that is strongest where it refuses to fill a
> gap, carry that restraint through delay and a new missing-input case, and
> finish quietly more trustworthy while the greater working world yields
> nothing in return.

## Player fantasy

The player is the expedition's **keeper of the claim boundary**: not someone
who makes the City disclose more, but someone who can tell exactly what a
human record is allowed to say.

The fantasy is:

- deliberately open a new expedition check after the Python work, rather than
  answer a summons;
- inventory which course inputs were actually supplied before describing an
  output;
- keep structure and provenance accountable to those inputs;
- notice when a tidy record is overclaiming and remove the unsupported claim
  without being handed a replacement;
- reconstruct the rule after the work surface has closed;
- carry it into a distinct audio/video missing-input case where the temptation
  to fill silence is strongest; and
- leave with a quieter, more durable discipline rather than permission,
  certainty, praise, or a changed world.

The achievement is a human one: the Pilot becomes harder to mislead by the
completeness of their own forms.

## Creative thesis: the honest empty place

The slice should feel like assembling a field ledger whose most important
property is that it preserves an **honest empty place** when evidence was not
supplied.

This is not an empty field waiting to be rewarded with the right answer.
It is a bounded, deliberate absence in an expedition-owned record: neither
false, negative, zero, denied, failed, nor an invitation to infer. The
experience becomes emotionally legible when the player feels that restraint
as positive craftsmanship.

The dominant contrast is:

```text
SC-04:
  immense, continuously operating, physically unchanged, still uncategorized

expedition claim ledger:
  temporary, source-accountable, wholly human, willing to remain incomplete
```

The ledger metaphor is presentational only. It does not create a new object,
case, evidence key, native Builder form, persistence model, or answer pattern.
Readable labels and status remain in the separate human interface. SC-04 never
supplies a prompt, source, media item, evaluation, repair, or acknowledgment.

## Attention hierarchy

Every phase has exactly one mounted owner/message/content/action group. Within
that group, the intended attention order is:

1. **Owner and complete phase heading** — the player first knows whether this
   is Pilot work, Teacher repair, or System pacing/status.
2. **Source boundary** — what the course has supplied, what form of output is
   being requested, and what remains unavailable, without exposing an answer.
3. **Current human responsibility** — the three equal dimensions, or the
   transfer dimensions plus its separately owned explanation.
4. **One valid next action** — submit, clear, retry, continue, or bounded early
   return as authorized by the shell.
5. **Local textual help/error/status** — near its control, concise,
   nonprivate, and subordinate to the work.
6. **The invariant greater whole** — visually dominant at shell scale and
   continuously present as context, never transformed into feedback.
7. **Secondary safe route** — visible only where fixed by the shell and never
   competing with the current responsibility.

The three extraction dimensions retain equal semantic weight. Size, hue,
position, iconography, lighting, motion, or sound must not imply which choice
is expected. The unsupported explanation is separately required at transfer
but must not be staged as a bonus, appeal, confession, or authority request.

## Pacing profile

The slice is a measured evidence drill, not a rapid assessment montage.

1. **Fresh entry — immediate and owned.** The Pilot action replaces the
   finalized Python group directly. No door animation, scan, handshake, or
   world cue precedes the blank primary.
2. **Primary — patient source accounting.** This is the first sustained beat.
   The player should have enough visual quiet to inspect supplied boundaries
   and complete all three dimensions without time pressure.
3. **Repair — brief, exact, and repeatable.** An actual miss clears work,
   names only what requires reconstruction, then returns through one explicit
   blank-retry action. Repeated repair stays equally calm.
4. **Interlude — one held breath.** The active work disappears. The System
   group is concise and permits a fresh continuation, but the pause itself
   supplies no evidence, fact, confidence, or ceremonial anticipation.
5. **Retrieval — leaner than primary.** The player rebuilds the three
   boundaries without the primary answer set. It should feel exposed and
   self-authored rather than visually harder or theatrically darker.
6. **Transfer — the strongest concentration.** The distinct audio/video
   missing-input case and separate explanation receive the clearest available
   working field. The challenge comes from ambiguity, not sensory noise.
7. **Landing — held stillness.** The final group is concise and has no action.
   It should remain long enough to read as complete without a flourish that
   asks the player to expect the next floor.

There are no timers, countdowns, progress sweeps, animated score counts,
success blooms, failure shakes, unlock beats, synchronized ambient pulses, or
delayed permission.

## Concept-level presentation direction

### Visual grammar

- Preserve maximum-quality first-person world presence, with the existing
  world plate occupying the greater share of shell-level perception.
- Evolve the accepted dark field-folio grammar into a **provenance ledger**:
  one restrained graphite surface with discrete source/accountability
  boundaries, generous negative space, and no resemblance to a native
  terminal, military targeting display, or familiar media editor.
- Use structure before color: owner heading, source envelope, equal-dimension
  field group, separate explanation boundary, local status, actions, and
  negative-authority note.
- A source envelope may use thin rules, material layering, or quiet inset
  planes to distinguish supplied, requested, and unavailable concepts, but it
  must not encode the evaluator's expected selection or reproduce a fixed
  answer arrangement.
- Avoid traffic-light semantics, completeness rings, radial gauges, progress
  bars, waveforms used as answer evidence, thumbnails that imply a supplied
  missing source, green/red grading, neon grids, holographic bloom, code rain,
  schema diagrams, checkmarks, locks, denied-state icons, eyes, doors, and
  native-looking Builder glyphs.
- Missing input must be expressed in persistent human text and semantics. A
  decorative empty aperture, dimmed media tile, or silent waveform cannot be
  the sole carrier of meaning.

### Material

- Reuse the interface's established charcoal-black, graphite, bone-white, and
  muted oxidized/mineral accent family.
- The ledger should read as low-gloss expedition equipment: fine matte
  composite, restrained metal edge, and shallow translucent separation only
  where text contrast remains strong.
- Use borders, spacing, rule weight, and surface depth to separate owners and
  states. Do not make failed work look cracked, scorched, contaminated, or
  denied.
- Empty fields have the same material dignity as completed structure. They
  contain no ghost answer, waveform, thumbnail, pseudo-writing, default
  selection, or heat residue.
- Forced colors must reduce the treatment cleanly to native/system boundaries,
  text, state, and focus without losing owner or error relationships.

### Color and light

- Keep SC-04's existing physically motivated warm-below/cool-vault balance
  unchanged throughout the chain.
- The claim ledger supplies only enough self-contained luminance for readable
  human work. It casts no visible light on the environment and receives no
  correctness-dependent reflection from it.
- Bone-white/neutral text carries primary meaning. Muted mineral or oxidized
  accents may distinguish structure but never success, failure, supplied,
  unavailable, or owner by hue alone.
- Required and failed states use text, native semantics, border structure, and
  focus destination before color.
- Pass, miss, clear, repair, retry, continue, retrieval, transfer, explanation,
  and finalization produce no glow, flare, exposure shift, temperature shift,
  vignette, world highlight, or sealed-boundary contrast change.

### Audio

- Add no runtime audio or media asset.
- The transfer's audio/video identity is conveyed through course-authored
  source labels and complete text equivalents, not playback, waveform
  spectacle, or a discovered city recording.
- Existing ambient world sound, if present in the accepted runtime, remains
  continuous, physically sourced, and identical across every group and
  result.
- There is no source-acquired chime, missing-input silence cue, pass sting,
  miss alarm, repair voice, reward chord, Machine voice, Builder voice, or
  environmental acknowledgment.
- An existing neutral control sound may be retained only if it is identical
  for ordinary activation, conveys no state/evidence distinction, and has
  complete silent parity. Silence remains the authoritative experience.

### Motion

- State changes use direct whole-group replacement. Motion may clarify that a
  human work surface was replaced, but cannot delay focus, reveal an answer,
  or stage evidence as a physical transfer.
- The interlude is primarily a compositional pause, not an animation. The
  interface may settle to a smaller System-owned boundary using existing
  means, while SC-04 continues only its already-authorized monotonic ambient
  operation.
- Submitted work clears before repair appears; no value flies, dissolves into
  the world, burns away, or travels to a persistent ledger.
- Retry opens already blank. Retrieval and transfer do not animate prior
  answers out of hiding.
- Reduced motion uses immediate replacement with identical content, order,
  status, focus destination, and evidence.
- No world motion starts, stops, accelerates, synchronizes, or changes phase in
  response to the player.

## Phase-specific creative guidance

### `CM-30` primary — establish the claim ledger

The first blank primary should make the new responsibility immediately
different from Python authoring. The player's task is not to produce code or
make a system run. The visible hierarchy should ask:

1. What course input is actually present?
2. What output structure/provenance obligation follows?
3. What must remain unsupported?

Those questions are a copy envelope, not final wording and not answer content.
They must remain equally weighted and wholly blank at entry.

### `CM-31` repair — narrow the breach, not the person

Repair is a human editorial intervention. Its emotional tone is:

> One boundary in this record needs reconstruction; nothing outside that
> boundary has changed.

Only the actual failed IDs and/or explanation boundary may shape the repair.
No repair language may contain an expected value, complete a field, imply
which option to choose, or characterize the player as careless. The prior
attempt is already gone when repair appears.

### `CM-32` interlude — close the ledger, keep the limit

This beat separates immediate recognition from durable recall. It should not
summarize the three answers or display a preserved result. The active group is
small, System-owned, and factual: the primary boundary is locally recorded;
continuation will open a fresh closed-note check; the world is unchanged.

SC-04 may receive more screen attention here only because the human work
surface is compact, not because the environment reacted. The environmental
clocks remain visibly and semantically unrelated to the pause.

### `CM-33` retrieval — return without residue

The retrieval group should feel more exposed than the primary, not more
decorated. There is no source-answer panel, previous selection, success color,
dimmed primary, or mnemonic icon. The same three responsibilities return as
fresh blank fields with their own concise labels and help.

### `CM-34` transfer — the disciplined refusal to invent

This is the emotional apex. The distinct course case involves audio/video
missing input, but the interface must not simulate a broken player, muted
track, denied microphone, inaccessible camera, corrupted file, or city media
source. Missing input is a statement about what the course supplied.

The three dimensions and separate unsupported explanation start wholly blank.
The explanation is treated as accountable human reasoning, not a password,
consent, appeal, access request, or confession. Finalization occurs only when
the entire current attempt passes together.

### Final no-action landing — close the human record

The final group should say, in final Quartermaster wording, only that
`RP003-IE-01` has been finalized locally and the course/world limits remain.
It has no button, next-floor hint, save language, review eligibility, bearing,
destination, score celebration, or status pulse.

The strongest finishing image is the invariant SC-04 world alongside a
settled, compact expedition status: human capability has become quieter and
more exact; the mystery has not moved.
