## One-sentence emotional promise

**Enter a place that makes analogy feel tempting, then earn the quieter
confidence of looking closely enough to leave your own conclusions behind.**

## Player fantasy

The player is not solving an alien test. They are the custodian of their own
method: arriving at an unfamiliar working surface, deliberately shielding
earlier conclusions, building one clean local account, and later reopening
the expedition's history without allowing it to overrule the new evidence.

The fantasy is disciplined fieldwork under uncertainty. The gratifying act is
not being declared right; it is maintaining separation among observation,
practice, provenance, records, and unsupported claims while the enormous
world remains indifferent.

## Creative thesis — the reach as unaligned laminae

The code-native world treatment presents SC-12 as a broad field of oblique,
overlapping laminae: translucent pressure-like planes, mineral-foam masses,
salt-metal seams, sealed former feeds, accreted margins, and repairs that do
not align into a road, grid, chart, or common horizon. Each layer belongs to a
credible larger process but refuses to become a diagram.

The visual thesis is **continuity without alignment**. Some relationships
persist across a material transition; similar-looking forms lead elsewhere;
a bypass maintains operation without explaining cause; one margin remains
unavailable. The six observations occupy equal compositional weight but never
form a numbered circuit or answer path.

The expedition interface uses a complementary thesis: **custody through
separation**. Fresh work occupies one clear central field. Prior records remain
visibly retained but unavailable as task content until finalization. When they
return, they open one at a time as complete bounded records, never as a
comparison wall.

## Emotional progression

### Arrival — exposed and deliberately quiet

The CF-30 route gives way to a wide, low-contrast field with no repeated
Counterfield landmark. The player first sees breadth, then several unrelated
material events, then the spare independent-record control. Nothing points,
welcomes, or wakes. The intended feeling is not danger; it is the slight loss
of confidence that comes when a familiar interpretive shortcut stops working.

The first action is an act of discipline: hide earlier records without
deleting them. The interface states this plainly and without drama.

### Development — close attention without a preferred order

UR-10 expands legibility rather than intensity. Six observation regions become
available as equal neutral responsibilities. Selection brings a local text
account and a restrained border/contrast change in the expedition overlay;
the world layer does not pulse, relight, align, or acknowledge.

The tension comes from resisting premature synthesis. Each observation adds
specificity while the whole remains unresolved. Revisit is calm and
idempotent, reinforcing that attention is not a score-combo mechanic.

### Understanding — the clean room of the fresh record

UR-20 narrows attention from world breadth to one sealed local work boundary.
The interface becomes quieter and more exact: strong labels, generous blank
workspace, direct feedback, and a visible reminder that replicas are local and
sanitized. The world remains visible as context but recedes in contrast, never
as a success backdrop.

The conceptual turn occurs after both learning chains and explanations pass:
independence is not isolation from history forever. It is choosing when
history is allowed back into the reasoning process.

### Reopening — history returns as bounded neighbors

At UR-30 the four prior records reappear individually. Their return should
feel like restored context, not reward loot or unlocked secret content. Each
record uses the same complete card anatomy and a stable provenance header.
Only one is active at a time; the other retained records remain summarized by
status, not visually ranked.

The reconciliation is deliberately slower and plainer than a comparison
board. The player walks through methods and explicit limits in a single
reading column. No connector line, matching animation, shared color, or merged
shape implies equivalence.

### Exit — careful confidence, unresolved horizon

The final save settles the interface into a clear custody ledger: fresh record
and reconciliation named separately, prior records still separate, unsupported
claims still unassigned. The world continues without event lighting or audio
resolution. The player leaves with confidence in a process and uncertainty
about the place.

LOOK returns the widest quiet composition in the slice but adds no visual
route. The outward bearing is present only as bounded negative space and exact
text, never a luminous path or ending cue.

## Attention hierarchy

The hierarchy remains consistent across all four phases:

1. **Current owner and exact heading.** The player always knows whose claim or
   responsibility is visible.
2. **One active responsibility.** One observation, form, explanation, record,
   reopen action, or reconciliation group is visually primary.
3. **Complete invariant-world context.** The reach remains broad and larger
   than the interface without competing with current text/control legibility.
4. **Status and provenance.** Persistent but secondary; never clipped or
   reduced to color.
5. **Returns.** Always findable, never visually urgent, and never confused
   with progress.

No bright ornament, animated seam, face-like symmetry, or high-contrast world
detail may outrank the active owner/heading. Equal observation regions use a
common control grammar and identical interaction prominence.

## World and interface relationship

The world and expedition interface never masquerade as one system.

- The **world layer** is Scene-owned: an invariant code-native laminar field
  with physically suggestive depth, material transitions, maintenance and
  unavailability. It contains no text, UI, status color, answer mark, route,
  or response.
- The **expedition layer** is owner-explicit: labels, records, forms, statuses,
  errors, returns, and save controls. It owns every learning and custody act.
- The **sealed Builder-work boundary** is a local interface frame nested inside
  the expedition layer. It reads as a supplied work replica, not a native
  terminal, lock, invitation, or live-world coupling.

World contrast may soften behind dense work to protect readability, but world
geometry and material relationships do not change. Hiding/reopening records
changes only the expedition layer.

## Code-native visual direction

### Composition

Wide layouts use a calm asymmetric diagonal sweep from lower near field toward
an off-center deep reach. Broad planes overlap without converging on a single
destination. The active world observation appears through local contrast and
an expedition-owned caption relationship, never an outline painted into the
world.

Narrow and 200% text zoom place a compact invariant-world header above the
active one-column interface. The world remains a meaningful place rather than
decorative wallpaper, but all responsibilities and returns fit without an
overlay covering the active control.

### Material vocabulary expressed in CSS

- pressure laminae: low-alpha layered gradients with variable border
  thickness and restrained refraction-like overlap;
- mineral foam: stippled/noise-free CSS texture made from small radial
  gradients, matte shadow, and irregular but deterministic edge radii;
- salt-metal seams: rough dark bands with restrained warm/cool specular edges,
  never neon circuits;
- ceramic anchor masses: weighty opaque planes with contact shadows and
  asymmetric burial;
- accretion and repair: thin offset layers, incomplete collars, and patched
  edges that imply several stewardship eras;
- unavailable margin: a materially complete opaque continuation that lacks an
  affordance, not a black locked door or disabled reward.

Do not use rasterized CSS, base64 images, canvas snapshots, SVG illustration,
external textures, generated noise files, or new media. Pseudo-elements and
gradients remain bounded and deterministic.

### Palette

The palette is restrained mineral atmosphere: graphite-violet depth,
smoke-green translucent laminae, pale fired-ceramic surfaces, muted salt-amber
contact edges, and cool condensation-gray. The expedition layer uses existing
high-contrast neutral tokens.

Color never carries a state alone. Selected, recorded, hidden-retained,
reopened, failed, saved, and held states each have explicit text, structure,
native semantics, and border/shape differences that survive grayscale and
forced colors.

### Lighting

CSS lighting suggests one broad motivated vault source, cool diffuse
condensation bounce, and restrained local thermal reflection. It does not
globally change after observation, pass, finalization, reopening,
reconciliation, save, restore, or LOOK. No bloom, answer glow, celebration,
alarm, synchronized emission, or invitation.

### Typography and record anatomy

Owner labels are compact uppercase custody marks. Headings are clear and
unornamented. Long status and provenance copy uses a comfortable line length
and visible paragraph rhythm. Record cards consistently separate:

1. record identity/provenance;
2. bounded observations/evidence status;
3. unsupported limits; and
4. available action.

Prior records do not use faded text that becomes unreadable. Hidden-retained
means a complete neutral custody summary with content unavailable to the
current work group—not a blurred mystery card. Reopened means one complete
accessible group, not four cramped columns.

## Audio and motion

No new audio file is authorized. The slice may inherit an already accepted
neutral ambient bed only if it remains semantically unchanged and its existing
identity is preserved; silence is the preferred fallback. There is no success
chime, failure alarm, route swell, save fanfare, synchronized rhythm, or audio-
only information.

Motion is optional and interface-local: a brief direct opacity/position settle
for replacement, a restrained focus transition, and slow decorative world
drift only if existing code can produce it without meaning. Reduced motion
uses immediate replacement and a static world. No world element reacts to
player progress.

- **TD-010 / Counterfield** used a broad lateral read of separately maintained
  districts and stewardship without standardization. TD-011 rejects district
  silhouettes and lateral collection. Its dominant read is overlapping,
  misaligned laminae with no shared destination.
- **Occluded Fold** centered bounded access and hidden relation. TD-011's
  unavailable margin is only one equal observation; the main drama is the
  player's custody of prior conclusions.
- **Offset Reach** used bounded relation and association. TD-011 avoids paired
  comparison framing and visible connectors; reconciliation happens in a
  single interface sequence after independent work.
- Earlier terminal and file-work slices localized attention on an object.

  sealed subordinate responsibility.

The code-native medium itself must not imitate TD-010's exact CSS silhouettes.
Use a new oblique laminar geometry, larger calm negative space, and a quieter
record-custody interface while preserving shared application identity.
