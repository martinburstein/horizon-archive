## Audience, player promise, and purpose

**Audience:** unchanged — an adult learner seeking real Python and AI-901
readiness inside an accessible, serious, first-person science-fiction mystery,
without requiring an account, credential, cloud resource, or network.

**Whole-product promise:** unchanged — investigate beautiful abandoned
Builder works, use real Python to interpret bounded local surfaces of the
Machine, and become more capable without collapsing the central mystery or
pretending to confer official exam, Microsoft, Azure, Foundry, service, or
world authority.

**TD-004 selection promise:**

> Select exactly one existing-authority, integration-sized playable segment
> that creates tangible forward player experience from the released boundary,
> preserves recoverable local play, and advances already-approved learning and
> mystery without manufacturing a destination, permission, response, or
> successor.

The segment must be a player-visible addition, not documentation churn or a
cosmetic substitute for progression. It may include only the learning,
observation, navigation, save, return, and presentation responsibilities that
the later strategic stages can trace to approved current authority.

## Six experience pillars — unchanged

1. **Real capability:** use real Python and independently verifiable AI-901
   evidence; traversal, presentation, observation, save, and Tour never become
   mastery credit.
2. **Bounded mystery:** expose evidence and relationships without defining the
   Machine, resolving Builder disappearance, or converting human categories
   into native fact.
3. **Recoverable rigor:** every miss, invalid predecessor, interrupted action,
   retry, return, reload, and resume has a deterministic honest boundary.
4. **Private learning:** retain only allowlisted finalized evidence; clear raw
   answers, prose, feedback, credentials, prompts, payloads, responses, and
   transient state at their defined boundaries.
5. **Accessible premium presence:** retain maximum-quality cinematic
   photorealistic first-person world dominance with keyboard, pointer, touch,
   focus, non-color, forced-color, reduced-motion, responsive, and effective
   zoom parity.
6. **Honest limits:** remain offline-first, local, no-credit in Tour,
   no-authority, no-exam-guarantee, and invariant-world unless an existing
   approved authority explicitly says otherwise.
