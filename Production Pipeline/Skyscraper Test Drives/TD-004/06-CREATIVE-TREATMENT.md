## One-sentence emotional promise

> The Pilot leaves a carefully verified local record for an immense working
> reach, learns to hold three visible relationships together without naming
> their purpose, and returns with a more exact expedition note while the
> greater system continues entirely on its own terms.

## Player fantasy

The player is a disciplined field observer at the edge of a civilization-scale
process. They are not decoding a control room or being admitted to a deeper
system. They choose to depart from a known, restored expedition boundary,
orient inside an unfamiliar reach, deliberately inspect three equally
important physical relationships, and resist the seductive leap from visible
convergence to purpose.

The fantasy is **becoming capable enough to describe a greater whole without
pretending to possess it**. The player earns confidence in their own method:
observe, distinguish, relate sanitized replicas through real Python, classify
course-owned AI workload cases independently, preserve only allowlisted
results, verify the local record, and choose a known return. The world does not
reward, acknowledge, grade, invite, authenticate, or answer them.

## Creative thesis: disciplined plurality

SC-05 should first overwhelm through coherent scale, then become readable
through three patient acts of attention. Its central dramatic idea is not that
three things become one. It is that **three distinct forms remain distinct even
where their visible paths appear to share a return**.

The slice therefore protects five separations:

1. three physical relationships are equal observations, not answer choices;
2. the apparent common return is a fourth observation, not a route;
3. visible correspondence does not establish native purpose;
4. Python and AI-901 work remain independent from each other and from scenery;
   and
5. a verified local save establishes expedition integrity, not world truth.

The emotional reward is disciplined uncertainty. The Pilot becomes more
precise and more accountable, not more powerful. The landscape's operating
rhythms, geometry, materials, light, maintenance, currents, coupling, and
common return never synchronize to player success.

## Attention hierarchy

Across the slice, attention should remain ordered as follows:

1. **The active owner and current responsibility.** The player always knows
   whether the Scene, Pilot, System, Builder work, or expedition course layer
   owns the visible claim or action.
2. **The exact physical or learning boundary in front of them.** Orientation,
   one deliberate observation, purpose-unknown tracing, one learning form,
   one explanation, review, save, restore, or return is primary.
3. **Three-equal-relation context.** Whenever SC-05 is visible, no current is
   privileged by brightness, color, motion, scale, position, completion order,
   or implied correctness.
4. **Provenance and limits.** Sanitized replicas, no live read/control,
   course-owned cases, local ownership, and purpose unknown remain legible
   before any success interpretation can form.
5. **One immediate valid action.** Required next work is clear without turning
   the interface into a command deck or score path.
6. **The apparent common return.** It remains visually important as a
   relationship to inspect, but subordinate to the three relations until all
   three are deliberately recorded and never presented as travel.
7. **Known safe returns.** Calibration Margin and City Threshold remain
   available where the shell permits, readable but not outcome-coded.
8. **The greater whole.** SC-05 dominates the sense of scale and continuity
   while remaining indifferent to interface state.

Completion order may change the expedition record but never the world's
visual hierarchy. Recorded relations remain peers; the first observed does not
become the leader and the last does not become the answer.

## World and expedition-interface relationship

### SC-05: the patient greater whole

The reach is one immense first-person working landscape rather than a room,
factory, utility plant, diagram, or corridor. Three materially different
handling traditions retain their own geometry and failure behavior while later
compatibility work and maintained-present repair fabric make shared service and
the capped return physically plausible. Inputs, transformation, distribution,
bypass, return, and maintenance remain traceable at contact, site, and
landscape scales without resolving purpose.

SC-05 is always causally alive and narratively indifferent:

- suspended particles continue through graded porous ceramic;
- cyclic pressure remains legible through tensioned span geometry;
- conducted heat remains legible through refractory joints and restrained
  refraction;
- low asymmetric maintenance forms follow fixed service seams;
- geothermal bounce, vault light, vapor, condensation, and material clocks
  retain their causes and cadences; and
- the broad glass-ceramic-capped return receives visibly distinct feeders and
  continues beyond the crop without becoming a portal, route, or revealed
  destination.

Arrival, observation, execution, misses, remediation, completion, save,
restore, and return change none of these. Any ambient event near a player
action is independent continuity, never feedback.

### Expedition and course layers

The expedition layer is removable human interpretation. It may select a crop,
name an observation, show a sanitized replica, expose a local status, and
retain an allowlisted record. It never looks fused into Builder matter or
borrows a native seam, glow, script, timing, or maintenance behavior.

The incomplete Python work image is ordinary `BUILDER WORK`, but the supplied
replicas, readable labels, execution boundary, feedback, and save meaning are
expedition/System mediation. It is not a terminal login, control surface,
authorization test, teaching machine, or live-current connection.

The AI-901 experience should feel like opening a compact expedition-owned
course instrument after the world relation has been folded out of view. Its
neutral cases share no crop, carrier texture, color, sound, motion, icon,
Builder referent, or spatial mapping with SC-05. The visual break is purposeful:
the player must feel that human workload classification is a different act
from observing the landscape.

The local record becomes quieter after verified restore, but never more
prestigious, luminous, architectural, or integrated with the world.

## Owner-separated answer-free language envelope

This section defines language intent, not final production copy.

### `SCENE`

May state only directly visible relationships:

- one physical carrier crossing one materially legible handling form;
- visible change in distribution, periodic form, or thermal gradient;
- three distinct feeder relationships entering one apparent capped return;
  and
- maintenance continuing along existing seams.

Scene language must use observable nouns and verbs. It may not say what a
system is for, which relation is correct, where the return goes, what the
Builders intended, or whether anything is aware.

### `PILOT`

Owns the decision to follow `TD004-RTA-001`, provisional expedition labels,
the act of recording, and cautious inference. Pilot language may join
observations into “different handling, apparent common return” only while
preserving purpose as unknown. It may not promote a label into native fact or
interpret uncertainty as denial, damage, invitation, or access.

### `SYSTEM`

Owns exact route availability, sensor-record status, sanitized-replica
provenance, first-incomplete recovery, local/offline transaction state, strict
restore integrity, clearing, and no-live-read/no-control/no-authority limits.
System language must describe expedition state, never city judgment. It may
say that a required relation, check, form, explanation, or record is incomplete
without exposing the missing answer.

### `BUILDER WORK`

Identifies only the origin of the bounded unfinished executable work. Human
readable scaffolding, safety constraints, supplied replicas, evaluator
feedback, and course framing remain expedition mediation. The work image never
addresses the Pilot, requests authentication, recognizes completion, grants
access, or signals why it exists.

### Expedition teacher/course layer

Owns AI-901 practice instructions, neutral answer-free repair, retrieval,
transfer, and the two separate explanation prompts. It asks for the requested
outcome and deciding signal without mapping a case to a current, material,
sound, crop, corridor, or Builder object. It never claims Microsoft, Azure,
Foundry, certification, exam, or production-service authority.

### Error and recovery tone

Errors are precise, local, non-punitive, and owner-specific. They identify the
relation the learner must inspect—such as iteration count, one append per
item, a case dimension, provenance, or purpose boundary—without giving syntax,
records, workload choices, expected explanations, or answers. A rejected save
names the first incomplete conjunct rather than presenting a score or general
readiness verdict.

## Concept-level direction

### Visual grammar

- Use the SC-05 panoramic thesis as the persistent world identity: three
  structurally distinct relations sweep horizontally from separate
  foreground/middle-ground origins into one deep, broad, capped return.
- Equality comes from comparable visual weight, structural legibility, and
  complete language—not three colored lanes, numbered choices, identical
  panels, or symmetrical answer slots.
- The porous field reads through particulate contact and graded voids; the
  tensioned relation through anchored load path and alternating compression;
  the thermal relation through joints, staining, expansion, and restrained
  refraction. Each remains intelligible in a still and grayscale.
- The common return is broad, low, materially heavy infrastructure. It must
  not resemble an aperture, tunnel mouth, eye, doorway, altar, road, abyss,
  light well, or invitation.
- The low embedded coupling is subordinate world evidence. It has no screen,
  pedestal, keyboard, handle, lock, seat, icon, face, or live-control affordance.
- The RP-003 material continuity remains a quiet lower-left return cue, never
  a sign, arrow, gate, newly lit path, or reward.
- Expedition interface states remain separate, restrained, and responsive.
  No world plate contains baked UI, text, symbol, cursor, highlight, or
  correctness cue.

### Material

SC-05 carries three visibly different material histories:

- dense porous fired mineral ceramic with residue, replacement plugs,
  condensation, abrasion, and believable particle contact;
- anisotropic ceramic-fiber laminates with anchored load paths, stress
  whitening, fatigue repair, and non-ornamental cyclic deformation;
- refractory stone-metal composite with expansion interfaces, heat cycling,
  oxidation, repair laminates, and physically plausible conduction; and
- a variable-thickness glass-ceramic return cap with refraction, internal
  reflection, strain, inclusions, repair fusion, mineral film, coherent
  contact, and believable weight.

Shared compatible collars and repair fabric may recur across all three
systems, but repetition must reveal interoperability rather than cloning.
Expedition materials stay low-gloss graphite, smoked clear ceramic, and quiet
neutral structure already compatible with the accepted interface language;
they do not imitate Builder material continuities.

### Color and light

- Dominant world light comes from geothermal bounce and a distant vault
  aperture, with physically plausible secondary scattering.
- Particles, tensioned edges, and thermal refraction receive different
  material responses without becoming saturated color identities.
- No relation is brighter, warmer, cooler, cleaner, more centered, or more
  emissive as a correctness cue.
- Observation, learning success, save, restore, and return add no world light,
  flare, beam, sweep, pulse, bloom, reflection, or color-grade change.
- Calm upper-vault and edge negative space protects scale and interface
  readability without hiding weak geometry in fog or darkness.
- Forced colors must retain owner, group, focus, action, error, disabled,
  recorded, saved, and return boundaries through system text and borders.

### Audio

No new runtime audio is required or recommended. If existing means can support
the scene without asset or budget expansion, atmosphere may consist only of
physically motivated, non-semantic continuity:

- granular carrier movement;
- low periodic structural pressure;
- restrained thermal expansion and distant circulation;
- sparse maintenance exchange; and
- quiet suit/System ambience.

The three relations cannot depend on sound for identity. There is no
completion chime, correct-answer tone, error alarm, Builder voice, Machine

Every state and action remains complete in silence.

### Motion

- Particle drift, tensioned displacement, heat refraction, condensation, and
  maintenance movement remain independently timed and physically causal.
- Motion may reinforce but never establish relation identity, order,
  availability, correctness, purpose, or evidence.
- Interface groups replace directly or use the smallest restrained local
  transition available within existing means.
- Recorded relations do not flow into the common return, connect into a
  diagram, merge, align, lock, brighten, or synchronize.
- Python execution never appears to travel into live systems.
- Save and restore never close a physical receptacle or cause a world event.
- Reduced motion uses registered stills or direct replacement with identical
  meaning, focus, status, and action availability.

## Phase-specific creative guidance

### CM-50 departure — chosen survey, not invitation

The new survey action belongs with two independent known returns. It should
read as one explicit Pilot navigation decision made from verified expedition
state, not as the city unveiling a path. Departure receives no response,
light, sound, opening, waypoint projection, or authority language. One valid
fresh intent cuts directly to arrival after validation; nothing is replayed or
written.

### `TR-00` — awe before categorization

Arrival should reveal the whole relational thesis at once: three distinct
handling systems, their shared landscape, the capped apparent return, a
subordinate sample coupling, and the known material continuity behind. The
player's first task is orientation, not solving. Equal observation
possibilities are legible, but no relation is selected automatically.

The emotional purpose is controlled awe. The player should feel the reach is
larger than the task and has been operating long before arrival. Early returns
remain calm, explicit expedition choices.

### `TR-10` — three patient acts of attention

Each observation is a deliberate close reading of one physical relationship.
The world view may select a registered crop, but it never isolates the relation
so completely that the greater system disappears or turns the crop into an
answer card.

The three interactions are equal peers. Completion order is player-owned and
has no dramatic arc of first/second/final correctness. After recording, a
relation may be marked in the expedition layer with complete text and
non-color structure; the physical world remains unchanged.

### `TR-20` — the discipline of not knowing

The common return is the slice's central mystery beat. The player has enough
evidence to see convergence and not enough to assign meaning. Presentation
should make the return compelling but materially capped, heavy, and
non-traversable.

The desired realization is: “I can preserve what I observed without filling
the blank.” Purpose unknown is not failure, absence, damage, refusal, or a
negative result. It is the correct boundary of available evidence. Review of
sanitized provenance may deepen that understanding at zero credit.

### `TR-30` Python — correspondence without control

The incomplete work image should feel ordinary, bounded, and workmanlike.
Supplied sanitized replicas and the empty output responsibility are clearly
expedition-mediated. The player writes one loop relation; execution remains
inside the local replica boundary.

Feedback names only the failed relation and returns a wholly blank responsible
form. Delayed retrieval and fresh transfer should feel like proving the method
belongs to the learner, not reproducing a privileged artifact. Finalized
Python evidence does not animate the landscape or pre-answer the AI-901 chain.

### `TR-30` AI-901 — a clean conceptual reset

The world/correspondence presentation folds away before the neutral workload
practice. This should feel intentionally plainer, not lower quality: a calm
course-owned instrument designed to remove accidental answer channels.

Cases appear without any visual echo of the three currents. Repair,
closed-note retrieval, fresh transfer, modality explanation, and agentic
explanation remain separately accountable. Passing the chain finalizes only
the named course evidence.

### `TR-30` review and atomic save — exactness without ceremony

Bounded review should expose three distinct responsibility groups rather than
one total: four physical observations, finalized Python, and finalized AI-901
including both explanations and authority/provenance limits. The player must
be able to identify the first incomplete obligation without a point score,
completion ring, rank, badge, or generalized verdict.

Save is one fresh Pilot commitment followed by a quiet System-owned local
transaction. It must not resemble uploading, filing into the city,
authentication, acceptance, sealing a native object, earning a key, or
unlocking an onward route. Failure remains calm, byte-preserving, and
deterministic; success replaces directly into verified restore after strict
read-back.

### `TR-40` — a dependable note in an unresolved world

Verified restore is the calmest state. It foregrounds local integrity,
expedition ownership, the three canonical relations, apparent-common-return
with purpose unknown, sanitized-replica provenance, independent learning
identities, and no-live-control/no-authority limits. Nothing replays.

Calibration Margin and City Threshold are known Pilot returns, not new rewards.
They should remain clear and write-free. An optional destinationless
continuation record, if retained, must look like a bounded expedition note,
not a disabled doorway, route teaser, or promise of RP-005.
