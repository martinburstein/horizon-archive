## Player fantasy

The player is a disciplined field archaeologist standing inside a material
assembly whose physical relationships are larger, older, and more patient
than the expedition's account of them.

The fantasy is **reading order without inventing history**.

The Pilot can:

1. make one fresh expedition-owned decision from exact released MF-30;
2. enter a works that neither invites nor responds;
3. inspect four equal physical responsibilities in any order;
4. distinguish supported sequence, visible change, continuity, and
   unavailable evidence;
5. use ordinary real Python over sanitized replicas while preserving source
   order and an explicit gap;
6. complete separate course-owned speech-capability practice without a world
   answer channel;
7. save one strict local note after every independent owner is complete;
8. verify that note without replay; and
9. return to a known released anchor.

The player does not reconstruct a past event, date the works, identify an
author, explain a change, open the closed interval, discover a native
category, prove a purpose, or affect the world.

The satisfying act is not solving the works. It is refusing to let a
defensible sequence become a complete story.
