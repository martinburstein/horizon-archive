## One-sentence emotional promise

**At a horizon that refuses to answer, discover that honest measurement can
end in either present readiness or a precise next step without turning either
outcome into a verdict on you.**

## Player fantasy

The player is the careful author of an expedition standard, not the subject of
an alien examination. They gather the record they truly earned, submit fresh
work without borrowing answers, accept exact gaps without shame, and preserve
one local decision whose limits are as explicit as its confidence.

The fantasy is mature self-command: the ability to say “ready here, by this
evidence” or “not yet, with these recoverable routes” while the immense world
remains uninterested in validation.

## Creative thesis - a datum held by the expedition

The unchanged SC-12 laminar world remains broad, oblique, layered, and
unresolved. It never aligns, opens, brightens, dims, or acknowledges a result.
The expedition interface introduces one contrasting idea: **a datum held by
the expedition**.

The datum is not a line in the world, a route, a targeting reticle, a gate, or
a native instrument. It is a quiet code-native interface discipline: stable
horizontal rules, consistent provenance headers, aligned text baselines, and
one current review column. The suit can hold a standard steady even when the
horizon supplies none.

The presentation metaphor is **an honest field folio**:

- five records remain individually bound and attributable;
- the reconciliation remains a separate leaf;
- current objective version and evidence coverage form a restrained colophon;
- fresh work opens on a clean, spacious central leaf;
- exact remediation appears as a narrow route index, never an answer key; and
- both outcomes occupy the same final leaf with identical scale and dignity.

This is not a dashboard, matrix, scorecard, certificate, transcript, exam,
tribunal, terminal, lock, or access instrument.

## Emotional progression

### Arrival - the world does not volunteer a conclusion

The player returns to the widest quiet reading of the Unborrowed Reach. The
new route action belongs visibly to the Pilot. On entry, the world remains
exactly where it was while a spare expedition folio assembles over it.

The first feeling is gravity without ceremony: all that work is present, but
nothing outside the suit calls it complete. Five named records and the
separate reconciliation appear as calm custody summaries, not trophies.

### Development - completeness becomes legible

MH-10 turns accumulation into responsibility. Each evidence family is
individually navigable, clearly versioned, and neither compressed into a
single percentage nor spread into an intimidating wall. The player can see
that completeness is made from distinct obligations.

An unmet gate does not trigger red light, alarm, collapse, or loss. The folio
opens one exact route tab in the same visual grammar as every other tab. The
tension is factual: a real gap exists and must be addressed.

### Fresh effort - the clean central leaf

MH-20 narrows attention to one generous blank work area. Prior records remain
present only as custody markers outside the active work region. The interface
is quieter than TD-011’s record-reopening phase: fewer layered cards, more
breathing room, stronger labels, and a single explicit sequence through
Python then current objectives.

The world remains visible as context but never as an answer, confidence cue,
or success backdrop. Passing and missing use the same physical presentation;
only exact text, native state, and route availability change.

### Remediation - precision without shame

MH-25 feels like placing one clean annotation in the folio margin. The exact
gap is named; earned evidence stays intact; the guidance contains no answer;
the next leaf begins blank. There is no punitive visual rewind, erasure,
descending rank, damaged artifact, or narrowing horizon.

The player should feel relieved by specificity: “I know what remains,” not
“the game has judged me.”

### Understanding - a bounded claim, honestly held

MH-30 presents ready and not-yet-ready in the same frame, typography,
contrast, world composition, and action hierarchy. Neither gets a seal,
fanfare, warning color, celebratory border, or failure treatment.

The moment of understanding is that a sound decision includes its scope and
limits. Readiness is confidence earned from evidence; not-yet-ready is a
valid recoverable state with exact work still visible.

### Exit - the datum remains local

MH-40 settles into a concise saved folio: decision, objective version,
evidence basis, remediation routes when present, and visible no-exam/no-
authority limits. The horizon does nothing. No route appears. No final light
falls. No Builder work acknowledges the player.

The exit feeling is quiet self-possession. The expedition can state what it
knows about the learner while still knowing almost nothing final about the
world.

## Attention hierarchy

1. **Exact owner and current heading.** Every claim begins with who owns it.
2. **One current responsibility.** Evidence check, fresh form, remediation,
   decision, or save is primary—never several competing panels.
3. **Invariant horizon.** World breadth remains visible and greater than the
   review without behaving like a verdict surface.
4. **Provenance and objective version.** Always legible, secondary, and never
   reduced to decoration.
5. **Exact limits and recovery routes.** Calmly available; never hidden or
   dramatized.
6. **Returns.** Consistent and findable, never visually confused with forward
   progress or outcome.

## World and interface relationship

- **World:** exact released code-native SC-12 laminae, Scene-owned, unchanged
  in geometry, lighting, rhythm, material, audio, and meaning.
- **Folio:** expedition-owned semantic layer for records, provenance,
  objective version, fresh forms, outcomes, remediation, save, and returns.
- **Builder work:** sealed sanitized-replica work region nested inside the

Outcome changes only exact interface text/state.

## Code-native visual direction

### Composition

Wide layouts preserve a dominant 16:9 world region and place one measured
folio column along the lower/near interface plane without covering the
laminar depth cue. The column uses calm horizontal rules and generous gutters
rather than card grids.

Narrow and 200% zoom use one natural column: compact invariant-world header,
owner/heading/status, current leaf, provenance, actions, then returns. No
evidence is hidden behind hover, side-by-side comparison, or spatial memory.

### Material vocabulary

- smoke-glass folio leaves with restrained opacity and physically plausible
  edge highlights;
- graphite ceramic rails used only as expedition structure, not world gates;
- pale mineral-paper fields for fresh work, with strong accessible text;
- muted brass-gray datum rules, never luminous route lines;
- fine stippled CSS gradients for depth, without raster texture, canvas, SVG
  illustration, data URI, or generated noise;
- stable provenance tabs with shape/text distinctions surviving grayscale and
  forced colors.

### Palette and lighting

The world retains TD-011 graphite-violet, smoke-green, ceramic, salt-amber,
and condensation-gray. The folio becomes slightly clearer and more neutral:
charcoal glass, warm ivory work fields, cool slate text structure, and muted
brass datum rules.

Color never means pass/fail. Both outcomes use the same palette. World light
never changes. Interface contrast may step between edit/review states only as
ordinary legibility, not emotion or reward.

### Typography and outcome anatomy

Owner labels remain compact uppercase custody marks. Headings are direct and
unornamented. The decision leaf always contains, in the same order:

1. local outcome string;
2. objective version;
3. evidence basis summary;
4. exact remediation routes, or explicit none;
5. no-exam/no-certification/no-authority statement; and
6. save and return actions.

Ready gets no larger type, icon, seal, or decorative privilege. Not-yet-ready
gets no warning icon, red treatment, collapsed content, or diminished action.

## Audio and motion
