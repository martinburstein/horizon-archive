## Player-facing content inventory

### Review hierarchy

The active review presents, in fixed order:

1. three Pilot-owned expedition observations;
2. finalized local Python conditional work;
3. finalized local information-extraction work;
4. exposed/supplied source provenance with an unavailable/unread boundary;
   and
5. an unchanged local/no-external-action boundary.

Every row retains visible state, source/owner, and a full sentence stating
what cannot substitute for it. No percentage, points, rank, aggregate,
progress meter, badge, correctness color, celebratory mark, or destination
appears.

### Save and recovery

- Save remains natively disabled and non-dispatchable before provenance
  inspection.
- Provenance review is described as zero credit and creates no evidence.
- The transaction copy says local/offline and complete-read-back rather than
  upload, handshake, acceptance, authentication, or native storage.
- Failure copy exposes only the safe consequence and fresh retry path. It
  does not expose storage bytes, exception text, tokens, private material, or
  diagnostics.
- Success directly replaces the transaction with the restored note; there is
  no ceremony, reward, or city response.

### Restore and return

- Raw note values such as `bounded_exposed_correspondence_observed` are never
  player-facing.
- The three human-readable note statements preserve their exact bounded
  meanings without teaching a native category or source value.
- The integrity statement verifies the local record only.
- Both returns retain their fixed visible action names and receive one shared
  descriptive association that makes prior knowledge, reversibility,
  write-free behavior, no replay, and no onward route explicit.
