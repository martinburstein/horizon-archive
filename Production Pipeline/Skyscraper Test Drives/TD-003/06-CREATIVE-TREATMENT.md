## One-sentence emotional promise

> The Pilot turns five separately earned truths into one dependable local
> memory, then discovers that successful preservation feels like quiet
> expedition discipline—not recognition from the immense city that continues
> unchanged around it.

## Player fantasy

The player is a careful field investigator closing one difficult expedition
loop with professional restraint. They do not receive a verdict. They inspect
the integrity of what they already earned, verify where every retained claim
came from, choose deliberately to preserve only what belongs in a local
record, and prove that the record can return intact.

The fantasy is **being trustworthy in the presence of an unknowable greater
whole**. The satisfying act is not gaining permission, unlocking a route, or
making the city answer. It is making human memory more exact while refusing
to mistake that exactness for native truth.

## Creative thesis: the quiet field closure

TD-003 should feel like closing a field case without closing the mystery.
The five obligations remain separate even when they become one atomic record:

1. observations;
2. `PY-010`;
3. `RP003-IE-01`;
4. provenance; and
5. no external action.

Their conjunction is the moral and visual center of the slice. Nothing is
flattened into a score, total, completion ring, rank, or approval. Provenance
inspection is a pause for accountability, not an additional achievement.
The atomic save is a deliberate Pilot act followed by a quiet System
transaction. Verified restore confirms only that expedition memory survived
intact. It does not validate a theory, settle a protected unknown, or prompt a
physical response.

SC-04 supplies scale and indifference. Its circulation, condensation,
maintenance exchange, light, sound causes, routes, coupling, sealed boundary,
materials, and clocks remain mutually unsynchronized and unchanged. The
expedition layer becomes more settled; the world does not.

correctness glow, confirmation chime, opening architecture, destination cue,
or physical response at any point.

## Attention hierarchy

Every state preserves this order:

1. **Current owner and phase** — `SYSTEM // EXPEDITION STATE`,
   `SYSTEM // LOCAL EXPEDITION TRANSACTION`, or
   `SYSTEM // RESTORED EXPEDITION NOTE`.
2. **The exact current responsibility** — review five independent rows,
   inspect provenance, save deliberately, inspect restored integrity, or
   choose a known return.
3. **Evidence ownership and limit** — expedition source, supplied/exposed
   provenance, unavailable-source boundary, and no-external-action meaning.
4. **One immediate valid action** — never a cluster of equivalent primary
   calls to action.
5. **Local textual status, help, or recovery** — quiet, bounded, and
   associated with the responsible control.
6. **Invariant SC-04** — always dominant enough to preserve scale, but never
   outcome-coded.
7. **Secondary known return** — available only where the shell permits and
   visually subordinate to the current responsibility.

The five review rows should read as peers in one ordered sequence. Their
individual independence must survive desktop, narrow, effective-`200%`,
forced-color, reduced-motion, and silent presentation. Do not arrange them
as radial progress, five lit modules, a scoreboard, a trophy wall, or a
single totalized seal.

## Pacing profile

Target clean duration remains `3-5 minutes`.

| Phase | Target | Creative rhythm |
|---|---:|---|
| Fresh entry and five-row scan | `30-60s` | Immediate orientation, then an unhurried top-to-bottom accounting |
| Provenance inspection | `45-90s` | The longest reflective pause; reveal ownership and unavailable limits without replay |
| Save intent and transaction | `15-30s` | One deliberate act, one compact moment of contained suspense, then direct replacement |
| Restored-note inspection | `45-90s` | Calm rereading with enough space to understand integrity and no-external-action meaning |
| Optional known return | `15-30s` | A quiet choice after completion, never a reward cadence |

Rereading is welcome and untimed. Focus speed, input modality, review order,
return choice, and time spent never affect evidence. Failed save or invalid
state should interrupt calmly and precisely: identify the first incomplete
boundary, clear private/transient work, and return attention there without
punishment language or ambient escalation.

## World and expedition-interface relationship

### SC-04

The city is the greater, older, active context. The same registered
first-person world presentation persists from entry through review, save,
restore, and return. The paired exposed bands, bounded difference, sealed
boundary, circulation, maintenance exchange, condensation, thermal depth,
coupling, return continuities, and environmental clocks keep their existing
physical causes and independent cadence.

No interface success may align with condensation, maintenance, light,
sound-source, crop, camera, material, route, or clock behavior. A transaction
completing during an ambient change is coincidence, not synchronization. The
world must never appear to receive, file, remember, accept, reject, judge, or
respond to the note.

### Expedition layer

The interface is a removable human interpretive layer, not a Builder console
or Machine organ. It should feel like a restrained field record assembled
from verified sources:

- the review exposes five independent obligations;
- provenance inspection exposes where retained evidence belongs and where it
  ends;
- save is an explicit Pilot decision;
- the transaction is System-owned and local;
- restore verifies integrity, not truth; and
- both returns are Pilot navigation choices to already-known anchors.

The interface may become **quieter and more compact** after successful
restore, but never brighter, more prestigious, more architectural, or more
integrated with SC-04.

low-gloss expedition graphite, smoked clear-ceramic restraint, fine neutral

Builder material seams.

SC-04 retains foundation mineral ceramic, variable-thickness glass ceramic,
oxidized functional alloys, fused repair fabric, residue, condensation, and
physically motivated atmosphere. The local interface does not borrow a
native seam, glow, script, coupling grammar, or maintenance rhythm.

## Phase-specific creative guidance

### Entry and `CM-40` review — five truths, not one verdict

The fresh review action should feel like opening an expedition accounting
view, not entering a chamber or being summoned. CM-40 presents all five
conjuncts in fixed order with equal structural dignity. The player should be
able to answer “what is complete, who owns it, and what cannot substitute for
it?” without seeing a numeric total.

The primary visual tension is between the finite, exact human record and the
enormous unresolved world. SC-04 remains visible and active but supplies no
status cue.

### Provenance inspection — the discipline of the edge

`REVIEW PROVENANCE` is the reflective center of the slice. It should expose
that retained Python and information-extraction evidence comes from accepted
exposed or supplied inputs, while audio, video, and sealed material remain
unavailable and unread. This is not an additional row, earned badge,
checkpoint, or lesson.

The action changes only the expedition presentation inside CM-40. It does not
replay source cases, observations, answers, feedback, confidence, or
completion. It unlocks no world element. After inspection, the player sees
why a save can now be attempted, not why the city has permitted one.

### `CM-41` atomic save — one quiet commitment

The save action is the only moment of deliberate commitment. The interface
should briefly state that a local expedition transaction is being validated
and preserved all-or-none. It should not depict a container, file flying
away, native receptacle, upload, network, handshake, authentication, seal,
signature, or acceptance.

Success is direct replacement by CM-50 after strict read-back. Failure is a
plain bounded System status and deterministic focus/return, with no red
alarm, shake, punitive language, or world change. If a last-known-good record
exists, its byte-stable preservation is a technical guarantee, not a
player-facing reward.

### `CM-50` verified restore — memory settles, mystery does not

The restored state should feel complete because its hierarchy is resolved:
record integrity, the three-part expedition-owned note, exposed/supplied
provenance, unavailable-source limit, and exact no-external-action meaning.
It contains no new result and replays nothing.

The quietest possible finish is preferred. The world is still operating on
its own terms. The Pilot has gained a dependable local memory and two
reversible choices to known places. No forward bearing appears. No control is
hidden or disabled in a way that suggests a forthcoming destination.

### Known returns — agency without unlock

`RETURN TO CIVIC COMPARISON` and `RETURN TO CITY THRESHOLD` should be plainly
named as known anchors. Neither is newly illuminated, privileged, or framed
as the “correct” continuation. Their hierarchy may be equal and secondary
after restored-note content.

An optional write-free CM-40 return may use the same ownership and restraint,
but it does not complete TD-003. Returns perform no save, replay, adapter
action, evidence change, city effect, or route unlock.

- TD-001 was the **quiet working margin**: Python fieldwork beside a vast

  distinct inside one local atomic record, then restore without world
  recognition.

The in-game emotional progression is more retrospective and deliberate than
either learning floor. Its focal action is preservation after inspection,
not answering, repairing, or transferring.

- steep landscape-scale independent clocks;

  sightlines, and ceremonial object arrangements.

The TD-003 reveal candidate therefore moves to a **mid-scale, low-oblique
object/world relationship** built around one slender physical braid rather
than a page, plate, container, interface card, archive opening, or another
landscape cross-section.
