## Player-readable rule

At every state, persistent text and native semantics identify:

1. the active owner and one immediate responsibility;
2. whether the world uses SC-05, the SC-06 panorama, or the SC-06 detail;
3. which of four equal physical observations remains incomplete;
4. whether recurrence is only description, divergence only supported
   difference, the sealed branch only unavailable, and stewardship only
   material continuity;
5. whether the active practice is Python, neutral text-technique work, one
   explanation, review, or local save;
6. the first incomplete or invalid boundary and its answer-free recovery;
7. whether local save is pending, failed with verified rollback, blocked by
   unverified rollback, or strictly restored; and
8. that no truth, quality, purpose, access, authority, reward, external
   action, exam guarantee, route response, or world response was created.

Image, crop, frequency, position, motion, sound, focus, modality, order,
timing, confidence, status, and save display grant zero evidence.

## Error, repair, retry, return, and resume

### Observation errors

Unsupported, forged, duplicate, wrong-owner, wrong-group, and combined
observation intents advance nothing. Public recovery names the first
unrecorded class only. It never calls recurrence normal/correct, divergence
abnormal/failed, the seal denied/locked, or stewardship owned/authorized.

### Learning repair

- Every first form and every retry is blank in visible, semantic,
  programmatic, and serialized state.
- Python misses expose only failed contract-check IDs and answer-free
  descriptions.
- Text-technique misses expose only failed case/dimension IDs and a bounded
  requested-output or confusion contrast.
- The whole responsible form is cleared on miss. Correct sibling selections
  are not retained.
- Repair has no answer, expected source/value, score, world crop, case-derived
  cue, or prior response.
- Retry is unlimited and uses a new event token.
- Private work clears on miss, retry, transition, return, reload, save
  failure, successful save/read-back, and completion.

### Same-session recovery

Same-session controller state may retain only finalized public evidence
records and completed observation IDs needed to focus the first incomplete
group. It may not persist partial forms in the RP-005 key. A gap truncates
that boundary and all later learning records. No record is recomputed,
duplicated, downgraded, synthesized, or cross-credited.

### Reload and restore

- Before exact verified RP-005 save, reload reconstructs exact TR-40/SC-05,
  clears RP-005 transient/invalid material, and requires fresh
  `TD005-RTA-001`.
- Exact malformed prior RP-005 bytes are not overwritten.
- Exact strict valid RP-005 raw/object equality plus exact unchanged TD-004
  bytes mounts MF-30 directly.
- MF-30 sets `replayedEvents=[]` and never replays route, arrival,
  orientation, inspection, observations, learning, explanations, review,
  save, sound, world, notation, or return.

### Safe returns

Exact Three-Current Reach and City Threshold returns are retained at every
player-actionable unsaved RP-005 group and at MF-30. They are absent only
during the synchronous transaction. An early return clears all
unsaved/private RP-005 state and performs no save. A return consumes only its
own fresh token. No direct Calibration Margin or Civic Comparison action
appears in RP-005.
