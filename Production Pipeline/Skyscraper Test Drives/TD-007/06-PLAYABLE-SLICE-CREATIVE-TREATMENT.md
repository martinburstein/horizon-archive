## Player fantasy

The player is a careful field archaeologist tracing two immense, incompatible
material continuities through a shared working region without forcing either
one into the other's category.

The fantasy is **recognizing relationship without inventing consolidation**.
The satisfying act is not connecting a network, solving a junction, or
discovering what the works are for. It is learning to say:

> These contacts recur. This local association is supported. The continuities
> remain distinct. The different contact remains ordinary evidence. The
> relative order remains noncausal. The junction interior remains unavailable.

The Pilot deliberately chooses the expedition route, observes five equal
physical responsibilities in any order, performs bounded replica-side file
work, completes independent course-authored vision/generation practice and
two explanations, saves one private-free expedition note, verifies it without
replay, and returns. Nothing in the region invites, recognizes, grades,
rewards, rejects, authorizes, opens for, or responds to the Pilot.

## Creative thesis - association without consolidation

SC-08 is one immense volume occupied by two separately traceable material
logics:

- a laminated glass-ceramic load ribbon defined by overlapping plate edges,
  tensile arcs, variable thickness, strain veils, and fused scars; and
- a cellular mineral-ceramic mantle defined by porous mass, capillary voids,
  compressive buttressing, sintered margins, and accumulated weight.

They cross repeatedly through saddle-shaped contacts whose compliant
interphases preserve both boundaries. Repetition makes a local association
credible. It never creates a central hub, synchronized signal, merged
silhouette, common emission, paired-system diagram, or native category.

One offset saddle carries a later quilted interphase, but remains equal to the
ordinary contact family. One exposed face supports relative order only. One
opaque off-axis junction preserves separate outer seams and a bypass while
revealing no shared interior. Foundation, adaptation, and maintained-present
repair coexist as compatible stewardship without naming an institution,
doctrine, author, owner, audience, or purpose.

The treatment locks these interpretive firewalls:

1. recurrence is not coordination, communication, dependence, or unity;
2. bounded difference is not error, damage, rejection, correction, or
   progress;
3. cross-cut order is not chronology, duration, authorship, intent, or cause;
4. closure is unavailable evidence, not a secret, refusal, lock, or reward;
5. compatibility is not consolidation, common ownership, or common purpose;
6. ordinary Builder work over sanitized replicas is not a live read, join,
   route, opening, analysis, generation, repair, or control action; and
7. a verified expedition record is local human memory, not world memory.

`Braided Verge`, `ribbon`, `mantle`, `continuity`, `association`,
`difference`, `order`, `junction`, and `stewardship` remain expedition
handles, not translated Builder terminology.

## Concept-level visual direction

### Composition

- Panorama lens territory: `28-34 mm`, first-person, landscape-first,
  kilometer-scale depth, calm lower-interface negative space.
- Contact-detail territory: `50-65 mm`, spatially registered, sharp enough to
  compare boundaries without becoming a diagram or abstract macro.
- Make ribbon and mantle independently enter and leave the crop at multiple
  depths.
- Distribute recurrent contacts through foreground, middle distance, and
  haze; reject radial, centered-hub, node-link, or prescribed-path layouts.
- Keep the different contact in the family rather than isolated.
- Keep the closed junction off-axis, opaque, and physically heavy, with
  separate peripheral seams and bypass only.
- Preserve foreground, site, and landscape scales, several stewardship
  strata, interaction readability, and responsive crop safety.

### Materials and grade

- Ribbon: variable-thickness laminated glass-ceramic with distinct plate
  edges, strain veils, internal reflection, inclusions, abrasion, fused scars,
  convincing Fresnel/refraction, and real weight.
- Mantle: dense sintered cellular ceramic with deep pores, sealed voids,
  compressed margins, thermal staining, condensation, dust, and patch fabric.
- Interphase: granular compliant material with compressed ridges,
  sacrificial wear, localized vitrification, and quilted repair, never a
  glowing connector.
- Junction: opaque fused technical ceramic and mineral accretion, no keyway,
  face, monitor, lock, door, or altar read.
- Grade: restrained cool mineral reflection with physically motivated
  geothermal warmth. Preserve shadow information and grayscale separation;
  avoid teal-orange shorthand, neon, reward glow, crushed blacks, or bloom.

### Lighting and atmosphere

Light establishes depth and material behavior, never correctness. A broad
vault reflection reveals ribbon planes; low geothermal bounce reveals mantle
weight and contact relief. Local emissions, if present in existing visual
language, remain restrained and unsynchronized. Haze, particulate fall,
condensation migration, and heat shimmer imply kilometers of depth without
concealing boundaries or artifacts.

No state globally relights. No peer receives an answer-key spotlight. The
different contact receives no warning color. The closed junction receives no
inviting rim or interior glow.

### Existing audio only

No new audio asset is authorized. Existing runtime ambience may be reused only
as low, physically motivated, non-semantic continuity:

- distant geothermal pressure;
- sparse structural settling;
- faint particulate or condensation movement; and
- restrained expedition-interface acknowledgements already present in the
  runtime vocabulary.

Audio never distinguishes the two continuities, counts or orders contacts,
marks the different contact, signals the junction, confirms correctness,
grades file work, identifies a capability, indicates save success, dispatches
a route, or suggests world attention. Silent play preserves complete meaning
and action availability.

### Motion and reduced motion

Motion is limited to monotonic, independent environmental clocks:
particulate drift, condensation migration, heat shimmer, strain settling, and
maintenance movement following existing interfaces. These motions do not
pause, converge, synchronize, point, acknowledge, or change with progress.

Interface state changes use direct replacement rather than spectacle.
Observation recording, learning success, save, restore, and return cause no
world animation. Reduced motion uses registered stills or direct replacement
with identical evidence, focus, status, crop, alternative text, and action
availability; `scroll-behavior:auto` remains exact.
