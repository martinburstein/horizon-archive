## Convergence lessons

- The model can reliably achieve either topology or material relationships and
  can occasionally achieve both in one base candidate.
- Large corrective edits are destructive even when the prompt says preserve;
  near-pass finishing should change one visible relationship, not three or four.
- The most successful topology is an open, shallow recession or fan, not a
  closed bowl, crater, rim, or barrier.
- The most successful material vocabulary is faceted charcoal load cells,
  solid ivory wedge-locks, and thin red offset lamellae with explicit
  over/under contact shadows.
- Treat dry access, red-scale discontinuity, and the red wear reaction as
  separate single-variable edit gates in any future authorized run.
- Do not rerun product tests until a source is actually accepted and imported.
