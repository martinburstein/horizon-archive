## Gameplay coverage rules

1. Every designed packet must state one player goal, exact entry and exit states, required/optional/unavailable actions, a complete interaction graph, expected duration, and player-observable acceptance evidence.
2. Mistakes must be recoverable. Hints preserve discovery; retries remain available; deterministic resume returns to the first valid incomplete boundary without replay or inferred credit.
3. Presentation, navigation, visibility, focus, acknowledgement, feedback display, timing, save, restore, Tour, and story progression never substitute for observation or mastery evidence.
4. Observation, Python, AI-901, review, and save evidence stay separate. An approved conjunction may gate progression but cannot cross-credit its parts.
5. Prefer one packet-level journey over one module or slide per sentence/state. Historical micro-seams remain regression evidence; new systems must reduce a named packet-gate deficit.
6. Every active world plus its current required owner/status/content/actions must fit one representative `1920 x 1080` shell at default zoom/text size without outer vertical or horizontal scroll, clipped ownership/copy, or an off-screen required action. The world remains visually dominant.
7. Narrow layouts and `200%` text zoom retain natural accessible vertical reflow. Required controls remain at least `44 x 44 CSS px`; names, labels, errors, focus, non-color meaning, forced colors, reduced motion, and supported modalities remain equivalent.
8. Save/resume stores only approved finalized evidence. Private/transient work, identity content, credentials, endpoints, payloads, responses, pointer/focus/token history, and external-action requests clear at defined boundaries.
9. Gameplay remains offline-first and no-authority: no live Azure/Foundry call, credential/resource access, city permission, access grant, consent, external action, or exam guarantee.
10. Demo Tour remains separate and zero-credit. It cannot own campaign dispatchers, evidence, scores, mastery, save eligibility, city state, or successor unlocks.
11. Reserved rows authorize no lore, location, puzzle, item, verb, Python skill, AI-901 objective, case, answer, threshold, route, art, code, or successor. They reserve sequence and gameplay-quality responsibility only.
12. Update this spine at integrated A4 graph lock, A5 protected smoke handoff, Gate Review, Working promotion, or accepted Demo status—not for individual transition micro-seams.
