## Locked premise

The player crosses from the prologue's distant City Beneath view into the first interactive threshold of a city that is visibly operating but not visibly inhabited. The slice distinguishes continued infrastructure, occupancy, and intent. The Pilot chooses to proceed; neither the city nor the Machine invites, recognizes, approves, or assigns a mission.

## Start state

- Predecessor end-state match: The three existing scenes are complete, the final Witness Corridor acknowledgement is retained, and the descent decision has been made.
- Player location: A first-person overlook at the City Beneath threshold.
- Required prior evidence/state: Accepted prologue completion and privacy-limited progression evidence; no working Terminal session carries forward.
- Visible world state: Heat, bridge lighting, maintenance movement, and environmental circulation continue across an immense empty civic landscape. No Builder occupant is visible.
- Available actions: Observe separate operating layers, establish a local expedition survey anchor, or return to the unchanged overview.

## Player goal

Establish a bounded survey point inside the City Beneath and identify a safe route forward without treating continued operation as evidence of welcome, occupancy, or awareness.

## End state

- Required state change: The expedition records a local survey anchor and exposes one reversible route toward the first civic-record district.
- Player understanding gained: A city can remain operational while the presence, purpose, and agency of its absent occupants remain unknown; utility access and identity-bearing record access are different visible boundaries.
- Next available action: `Enter the civic district`.
- Facts that remain unresolved: Why the city operates; what `continuation` identifies; whether any current process is attentive; whether access boundaries are technical, civic, ethical, ceremonial, or several at once.
