## Disposition

**`FIRST RUN VISION BASELINE / FRPB-001-v1`**

The released working game can become a fully fledged Horizon Archive without a
new product category, story branch, lesson, packet, platform, lore answer, or
ending. The work is product completion: make the complete approved journey
feel complete from a new player's first input through the unchanged Measured
Horizon.

## Audience

The primary First Run player is one person playing locally in a modern desktop
or mobile-class browser who:

- may have zero Python experience;
- is preparing against the current AI-901 learning objectives but must not be
  promised an exam result;
- is willing to learn through practice, error, remediation, and fresh transfer;
- is attracted to slow-burn first-person science-fiction mystery;
- may use keyboard, pointer, touch, zoom/reflow, reduced motion, forced color,
  or assistive semantics; and
- expects mistakes never to make the story unwinnable or lesser.

The game must also remain truthful for a returning player restoring a sanitized
local expedition and for a player who reaches NOT YET READY rather than READY.

## Complete-game player promise

Enter an abandoned alien city on foot, learn to recognize and connect to forty
physically distinct local surfaces through one consistent expedition interface,
build real Python and AI-901 method through recoverable practice, and finish
with an honest measure of current readiness while the city preserves its
mystery and refuses to judge you.

The player should feel increasingly capable without feeling increasingly
entitled to an answer. The final emotional reward is earned restraint, not a
world unlock.

## Observable meaning of “fully fledged”

Horizon Archive is fully fledged when a sanctioned clean-start playthrough and
sanctioned restore path demonstrate all of the following:

1. **Finished entry.** Product naming, launch copy, title, setup, onboarding,
   and the first playable scene contain no temporary or contradictory production
   language and make the first action understandable.
2. **Coherent journey.** Every required location, transition, Terminal cluster,
   and act turn has an approved runtime expression or an explicit accepted
   compression; nothing essential exists only in planning prose.
3. **Trustworthy play.** Actions, unavailable states, feedback, retry, return,
   save, restore, and malformed-state recovery are deterministic, recoverable,
   and clear.
4. **Real learning.** Python and AI-901 practice remains correct, current,
   answer-free in remediation, genuinely fresh in transfer, independent in
   evidence, and visibly connected to player method without becoming lore.
5. **Authored content.** Player-facing labels, instructions, dialogue, feedback,
   credits, and ending copy are final-purpose, source-safe, accessible, and
   consistent in voice.
6. **Cohesive presentation.** Existing world media, interface, typography,
   sound, motion, pacing, and code-native effects form one intentional first-
   person experience rather than adjacent production increments.
7. **Equivalent access.** Keyboard, pointer, touch, semantic activation,
   assistive naming, focus, narrow reflow, effective `200%`, forced color, and
   reduced motion preserve action and meaning.
8. **Production reliability.** Supported local launch, build, loading,
   performance, offline behavior, save lifecycle, error handling, tests,
   validators, served identity, E2E, and cleanup pass at release tier.
9. **Honest ending.** READY and NOT YET READY remain equal local outcomes, the
   same horizon follows both, and no effect implies certification, permission,
   world recognition, or a successor.

`FIRST RUN COMPLETE` is a release classification over this complete evidence,
not a declaration that no future refinement is imaginable.

## Experience pillars

### 1. Wonder with physical causality

Every location should feel unprecedented, immense, and beautiful because its
materials, flows, maintenance, failures, and history make sense. Mystery must
not become decorative randomness.

### 2. One interface, forty discoveries

No two physical Terminal hosts are exactly alike; the suit-rendered human
interface remains consistent. Discovery should make the player feel observant,
not like they are walking between kiosks.

### 3. Competence without humiliation

Errors are useful states. Feedback identifies the actual gap, remediation does
not expose the next answer, and fresh work lets the player demonstrate growth.
Failure affects time and practice, never story legitimacy or dignity.

### 4. Ownership without ventriloquism

Scene owns observation, Pilot owns interpretation, and Suit/System owns
interface and evidence. The Machine and Builders do not speak. Presentation may
deepen emotion but cannot make the city answer in the player's voice.

### 5. A trustworthy expedition

The player can understand controls, leave and return safely, restore exact
progress, recover from malformed or incomplete state, and know what local work
does and does not mean.

### 6. Restraint as payoff

The game earns silence. The final unchanged horizon should feel like completion
because every earlier scene taught the player to separate capability from
authority and evidence from desire.

## Quality bar

The quality target is a premium contemporary first-person science-fiction
learning adventure, not an educational form with scenic backgrounds and not a
visual novel laid over tests.

- **Product:** a new player can understand what they are doing and why without
  reading repository documentation.
- **Narrative:** each location changes the Pilot's understanding and hands a
  physical/motivational line to the next.
- **Interaction:** primary actions are readable, semantic, reversible where
  permitted, and never ambiguous about consequence.
- **Learning:** exercises are current, technically correct, bounded, accessible,
  and meaningfully practiced.
- **Content:** no accidental scaffold, stale demo promise, generic filler,
  misleading placeholder, or unowned voice reaches the player.
- **Presentation:** existing media and code-native treatment meet the strongest
  feasible cinematic hierarchy without falsifying unsupported asset work.
- **Engineering:** deterministic source/state ownership, robust failure paths,
  production builds, performance budgets, and regression evidence are release
  conditions.
- **Accessibility:** equivalent meaning and action survive every supported
  modality/layout/state; honest unavailable AT evidence is recorded.

## Platform and offline assumptions

- Primary distribution remains a local browser game launched on Windows from
  the project package.
- The responsive browser runtime supports representative desktop and narrow
  layouts rather than fixed pixel canvases.
- Current Node.js LTS/npm availability may be an installation prerequisite;
  normal play must require no development knowledge.
- Campaign and lesson work remain offline. No Azure/Foundry account, service,
  network call, endpoint, credential, or resource is required.
- Progress remains in one sanitized local browser slot unless a later
  Martin-approved product decision changes platform/storage scope.

## Scope

First Run may:

- replace temporary/stale player-facing production language with approved copy;
- improve clean-start and restore onboarding;
- reconcile runtime expression with approved game-on-paper chapters, hosts,
  transitions, voices, and ending within one bounded Work Order at a time;

  content, existing-asset usage, sound, motion, CSS treatment, performance,
  build reliability, fixtures, tests, and documentation;
- refactor shared runtime architecture when a shell proves the player value and
  regression radius; and
- disposition an intentional compact representation honestly when full
  expansion is not required for the player promise.

## Non-goals

- A sequel, RP-013, alternate path, optional canon branch, new ending, or
  post-credits hook.
- A new AI-901 objective, lesson owner, score, threshold, certificate, or live
  service action.
- Solving the Machine, Builders, disappearance, identity, consciousness, or
  continuity mystery.
- Changing the game into a ship/planet travel structure, combat game, open
  world, multiplayer product, account service, or cloud application.
- Replacing the first-person point-and-click learning-adventure identity.
- Generating/editing/replacing/importing images or publishing cycle reveals
  under the current boundary.
- Treating the game-on-paper, concept references, or proposed dialogue as
  already integrated runtime evidence.
- Pursuing decoration while an earlier material first-run gap remains.

## Priority rubric

Operations must rank candidate gaps in this order unless it records a justified
dependency exception:

1. destructive, privacy, save, evidence, hard-stop, or unrecoverable failure;
2. critical accessibility or input-equivalence failure;
3. clean launch, title/setup, onboarding, and first-success confusion;
4. missing/contradictory canonical journey expression or route continuity;
5. unclear learning instruction, feedback, remediation, or outcome meaning;
6. player-facing temporary copy, placeholder, missing content, or stale product
   claim;
7. pacing, repetition, sensory hierarchy, and audiovisual cohesion;
8. responsive polish, performance, loading, optimization, and lower-severity
   friction; and
9. optional embellishment that does not advance a required maturity dimension.

Within the same severity, choose the earliest clean-start address. A shared
global correction may move first only when it is necessary for the earliest
address or prevents duplicate/destructive work.

## Product-level success signals

The program may propose `FIRST RUN COMPLETE` only when independent release
evidence shows:

- one sanctioned clean start reaches the shared ending with no runtime error,
  temporary player-facing copy, broken action, or undocumented route gap;
- one sanctioned exact restore resumes at representative incomplete and
  complete boundaries without replay, leakage, or corruption;
- title/setup/onboarding/first Terminal can be understood and completed by a
  new player without repository knowledge;
- every game-on-paper location/Terminal cluster has accepted runtime expression
  or a recorded non-blocking compression classification;
- all required learning mappings and validators pass against current authority;
- answer-free remediation, fresh retry/transfer, evidence independence, and
  privacy clearing remain exact;
- every critical player surface is `FR4 RELEASED` on the First Run Scoreboard;
- full tests, production/fixture builds, served identity, performance budget,
  complete non-overlapping E2E, responsive/accessibility states, runtime logs,
  cleanup, and Git synchronization pass at final release tier;
- media inventory matches explicit authority and no forbidden image operation
  occurred; and
- READY/NOT YET READY converge on the unchanged horizon with
  `successor=null`.

## Reopen triggers

Reopen this product baseline only when:

- Martin changes audience, platform, product category, educational promise,
  media authority, visual ceiling, or ending;
- current official AI-901 objectives materially change the complete-game
  educational promise;
- an independently released First Run discovery proves a global product-level
  contradiction;
- supported local-browser/offline assumptions become infeasible;
- the whole-game completion sweep proves the finish signals insufficient or
  mutually incompatible; or
- Intelligence recommends `REDESIGN` with reproducible evidence and Martin
  authorizes the product-level change.

Local bugs, content gaps, presentation variances, and Work Order discoveries do
not reopen the baseline by themselves.
