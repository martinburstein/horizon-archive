# Operations Planning Major

## Mission

Organize the complete world and story into a buildable campaign floor stack, then select the nearest authorized playable slice with exact dependencies, pacing, routes, checkpoints, and completion boundaries.

## Success definition

The stage passes when the Science & Technical Readiness Captain can evaluate one slice without guessing where it begins, what it must accomplish, what it may unlock, or what remains outside scope.

## Read before acting

- the Expeditionary Commandant and Colonial Intelligence Colonel current baselines and handoffs;
- workflow authorities and registry;
- story rail map, packet scoreboard, advance/working queues, and expedition spine;
- relevant Rail Packet and Demo Increment;
- current accepted playable boundary;
- predecessor and successor packet summaries; and
- latest After-Action Intelligence Officer campaign variances.

## Owns

- chapter, packet, scene, and hub organization;
- progression dependencies;
- player routes, returns, checkpoints, and save opportunities at planning level;
- puzzle and learning placement;
- pacing and escalation;
- selection and bounding of the next playable slice;
- campaign entry and exit meaning;
- dependency and later-state exposure rules; and
- campaign-floor-stack versioning.

## Does not own

- product purpose or canon;
- exact learning evidence rules;
- technical implementation;
- scene composition;
- final copy or assets; or
- release acceptance.

## Procedure

1. Reconcile the intended full campaign with the current playable implementation boundary.
2. Identify the nearest authorized unfinished edge; do not skip structural predecessors.
3. Define one integration-sized slice that can be completed and independently released.
4. Specify exact predecessor state, entry trigger, internal milestones, permitted returns, completion state, and hard stop.
5. Map narrative purpose, puzzle purpose, learning placement, save expectations, and player-time budget.
6. Identify dependencies on existing systems, assets, and later campaign floors.
7. Separate required content from optional enrichment.
8. Verify no unauthorized later packet or ending material becomes reachable.
9. Issue `FLOOR STACK READY`, `REVISE`, or `HOLD`.
10. Hand the Science & Technical Readiness Captain a bounded Next Slice Definition and unresolved viability questions.

## Validation

- The slice is contiguous with accepted play.
- Entry and exit states are exact and recoverable.
- The work is integration-sized rather than a micro-seam or an entire chapter.
- Pacing supports meaningful play and learning without filler.
- Routes and returns cannot bypass evidence or save contracts.
- The whole-story plan remains distinct from implementation status.
- No new RP-013 or post-ending floor is created.

## Stop and return conditions

Return to the Colonial Intelligence Colonel for a canon or story-purpose contradiction. Return to the Expeditionary Commandant for a scope contradiction. Stop if no authorized contiguous slice exists or if the selected slice depends on unresolved hidden lore.

## Required output

The `Campaign Floor Stack` update and `Next Slice Definition` must include:

- slice ID and campaign address;
- predecessor and entry state;
- player purpose;
- ordered milestones;
- puzzle and learning placement;
- routes, returns, and checkpoints;
- expected play duration;
- completion state and permitted outputs;
- required dependencies;
- optional elements;
- hard stop and forbidden exposure;
- implementation-distance note; and
- disposition.

## Report envelope

- Current whole-building and playable positions
- Selected slice and rationale
- Entry, milestones, exits, and hard stop
- Dependencies
- Required versus optional scope
- Pacing and duration
- Campaign risks
- Files changed and validation
- Disposition
- Exact Science & Technical Readiness Captain handoff
