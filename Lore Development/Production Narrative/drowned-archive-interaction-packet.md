# Drowned Archive Interaction Packet

Status: production-ready, surface-canon only.

Scene contract: `AB-01` is a flooded basin with two deliberately different objects. The grounded three-fin **Workload Sort Terminal** is a bounded local interface. The monumental **suspended archive** is a silent environmental landmark. Completing the exercise may change the Terminal, its reflection, and the onward route; it must not make the landmark speak, rotate, approve, awaken, or explain itself.

## Production alignment

- Scene sheet: `Concept Art Book/scenes/AB-01-drowned-archive-workload-terminal.md`
- Exercise: `EX-L0201-WORKLOAD-SORT`
- Lesson: `L-02-01`
- Activity: `A-L0201-2`
- Objective: `AI901-D1-O4`
- Skills: `AI901-D1-O4-WORKLOAD-MATCH`, `TERM-STATE-001`
- Visual target: grounded three-fin node at the causeway apron; suspended geometric frame above the central island.

The workload categories are supplied by the human expedition curriculum. Their successful use does not prove that the Builders used the same categories. The Terminal accepts a bounded classification record through the mediation layer; the exact compatibility remains `UNKNOWN`.

## Object-separation rule

| Object | What the player can support | Allowed response | Forbidden implication |
|---|---|---|---|
| Grounded Terminal | A local node emits light, opens the human workbench, retains a local session, and changes state after transfer | Fin light, reflection, status language, route cue | That it is the whole Machine, a person, or a native AI-workload tutor |
| Suspended archive | A large geometric structure remains above the island without a readable access surface | Observation, comparison, authored silence | That it asked for identity, graded the lesson, watched the player, or approved the result |
| Basin / causeway | Reflections and route lighting can connect local events spatially | Water light, paving seams, distant echo | That every environmental change is deliberate communication |

The player should leave knowing more about the **difference between an operable surface and an observed unknown**, not more about the landmark's purpose.

## Entry beat

`SCENE`:

> The causeway ends at a three-fin node set into the paving. Beyond it, a dark geometry hangs above the flooded island. The small thing offers an interface. The large thing offers only scale.

Evidence: the Terminal is accessible through the expedition link (`OBSERVED`); the landmark has no detected access response (`OBSERVED`). Do not label the landmark dormant, dead, aware, or inactive. Silence is a measurement, not a diagnosis.

Tags: `OBSERVED` for visible geometry and survey result. “Archive” remains a human survey name, not a confirmed Builder label.

`EXPEDITION LINK`:

`PILOT // FLIGHT RECORDER`:

This line prevents the player from assigning the Terminal's response to the landmark.

## Narrative-purpose summary

The encounter teaches the player to classify bounded tasks while the scene refuses to classify its largest object. That parallel should remain implicit in play and explicit in production:

- Learner action: choose the best primary category from evidence.
- Character growth: resist applying a category where evidence is insufficient.
- World response: the verified local surface changes and marks a route.
- Preserved mystery: the monumental landmark stays materially present, operationally silent, and unexplained.

## 901 Teacher handoff for `L-05-07`

Build the final reference lesson around a return to the Drowned Archive with a **multimodal evidence packet** containing three source types: a still image of the suspended landmark, a short basin-audio segment, and Terminal/environment telemetry. The learner should:

1. define a small extraction schema with `source_id`, `modality`, requested fields, and uncertainty;
2. distinguish general visual description from information extraction into named structured fields;
3. preserve a null or absent field instead of inventing a landmark response;
4. inspect the structured result while keeping source evidence and interpretation separate.

Alignment: `L-05-07`, `AI901-D2-O7`, `PY-015`, `PY-016`, `PY-020`; prerequisites `L-04-04` and `L-05-03`. Use the official Content Understanding overview registered as `OFFICIAL-CONTENT-UNDERSTANDING` for current product claims. Keep the landmark's silence as missing/absent evidence, never as proof of dormancy or consciousness.

Recommended assessment contrast: “Describe what is visible in this image” versus “return `structure_count`, `access_surface_detected`, `audible_response_detected`, and source references from the supplied image, audio, and telemetry.” The first is a broad vision task; the second is a schema-driven multimodal information-extraction flow.

## Validation gates

- The grounded node owns all exercise access and response language.
- The suspended archive never speaks, grades, rotates, awakens, or receives a human identity.
- Workload labels remain human curriculum categories.
- Close/reopen retains the active form; transition clears working state but not mastery evidence.
- Hints and remediation do not change story access or unlock unique canon.
- Success changes the Terminal, reflection, and route—not the landmark.
- Every Machine-adjacent line states condition or relation, never motive.
- `L-05-07` bridge preserves absent fields and provenance without making product claims beyond the official source.
