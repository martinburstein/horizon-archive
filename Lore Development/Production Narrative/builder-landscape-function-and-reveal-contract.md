# Builder Landscape Function and Reveal Contract

Status: project-wide surface-canon production rule. This file does not define hidden canon.

## Non-negotiable surface canon

1. Every Builder landscape is functioning or was built to function. Resource acquisition, material cultivation, planetary transformation, computation, transport, preservation, coordination, stewardship, or another rigorous occupation is embedded in the terrain.
2. Builder artifacts were not designed for human bodies, senses, time scales, assumptions, or ergonomics. Initial incomprehension is expected; a familiar handle, room, screen, or profession must never be the default reading.
3. Alien does not mean random. Shapes, intervals, omissions, responses, and failures follow stable relations even when the pilot cannot yet perceive the governing variables.
4. The Builders are more advanced than humanity as of 2026 in every relevant comparison the story presents. Damage, age, silence, or partial access must not be used to imply technical parity or primitiveness.
5. The protagonist is the first known human to encounter each Builder instance. Player-facing Pilot language is singular—`I`, `me`, and `my`. Do not invent a party, crew, prior human survey, or shipboard witness.
6. The Machine may expose a human-readable mediation surface, but that does not make its native form human-oriented or prove it anticipated humanity.

## Landscape occupation grammar

`Occupation` is a provisional human translation for a repeatable landscape-scale function. It is not a Builder job title, cultural caste, consciousness claim, or final explanation.

Every environment production packet must contain:

- `OBSERVED INPUTS`: matter, energy, records, signals, environmental gradients, or other directly sensed feedstock;
- `OBSERVED TRANSFORMATIONS`: repeated changes, routing, sorting, growth, computation, calibration, or maintenance;
- `OBSERVED OUTPUTS`: material, environmental, informational, logistical, or preservation effects;
- `STEWARDSHIP EVIDENCE`: repair, rejection, redundancy, thresholds, recovery, conservation, or long-duration regulation;
- `PROVISIONAL PILOT OCCUPATION`: one compact human gloss plus at least one viable alternative;
- `HARD WITHHOLDING`: the native Builder category, ultimate purpose, operator identity, and relationship to consciousness.

Allowed broad working families include resource acquisition, silicate/material cultivation, planetary transformation, computation, civic stewardship, transit/logistics, evidence preservation, and network coordination. They may overlap. Never force a landscape into one human industry when repeated evidence supports a coupled system.

Preferred phrasing:

> I am calling this cultivation because material enters, trained forms grow, rejected pieces are sorted, and repairs reuse the same feedstock. That is my occupational gloss, not its name.

Avoid:

> It is obviously a farm.

## Four knowledge lanes

Keep these lanes separate in source notes even when player-facing prose omits labels:

1. `OBSERVED`: direct sensory or instrument evidence. Scene copy may state it without motive.
2. `PILOT_INFERENCE`: the protagonist's first-person interpretation, including every occupation gloss. It must admit uncertainty or alternatives when the category is not established.
3. `901_TEACHER_EXPLANATION`: human-authored curriculum instruction and sourced Microsoft terminology. It may explain the safe exercise, never the true Builder function or motive.
4. `HIDDEN_CANON`: author-only truth, if any. It never enters surface-canon files, Teacher copy, validator feedback, accessibility text, filenames, metadata, or progress reports.

`BUILDER_LABEL`, when recovered, remains a provisional translation artifact between observation and inference. It does not authorize the Teacher or Pilot to collapse the four lanes.

The legacy production tag `HUMAN_INFERENCE` means singular `PILOT_INFERENCE`; it does not imply an unseen human group.

## Functional reveal ladder

Reveal occupation through accumulation, not omniscient exposition:

1. **Alien encounter:** present two concrete sensory facts and one missing human affordance. Do not call the object random, magical, decorative, broken, or purposeless merely because its use is unclear.
2. **Repeated relation:** show the same input/transformation/output relation at least twice, preferably through different positions, times, or modalities.
3. **Stewardship trace:** expose a repair, rejected output, threshold, reroute, conservation behavior, or recovery cycle proving rigor beyond spectacle.
4. **Safe Terminal exercise:** let the pilot reproduce or classify one bounded relation through human tooling. The exercise reveals evidence; it does not translate the whole landscape.
5. **Provisional occupation:** allow a first-person human gloss only after the evidence above. Keep an alternative live.
6. **Scale echo:** repeat the relation at object, architectural, environmental, or planetary scale without claiming identical purpose or a conscious coordinator.

A new reveal is valid only if it answers one operational question and leaves the native category, ultimate purpose, and agency unresolved.

## Advanced-technology portrayal

- Demonstrate advancement through precision, durability, integration, scale, recovery, and preservation of uncertainty—not decorative complexity or omniscient technobabble.
- Human tooling may expose only a narrow compatibility layer. A successful Python or AI-901 exercise proves that bounded mediation works, not that Builder infrastructure runs on human software.
- Do not make a Builder process fail merely to flatter human ingenuity. The pilot succeeds by observing rigor, forming testable models, and using safe interfaces.
- Human categories should feel useful but lossy. The closer an occupation seems to a familiar farm, archive, factory, computer, or utility, the more the production notes must preserve what that analogy omits.
- Never resolve incomprehensibility with magic, dream logic, arbitrary symbol changes, or a late exposition dump.

## First-contact and accessibility rules

- Each instance has no prior known human visitor, annotation, footprint, translation, or ergonomic accommodation unless a later authorized reveal explicitly changes that surface fact.
- Pilot dialogue stays first-person singular. System and Teacher copy may address `you`; neither implies a crew.
- Accessible labels and text equivalents describe observed geometry, state, and interaction purpose. Accessibility is human mediation, not evidence that the Builder object was designed for human senses.
- Incomprehensible native form is never an excuse for an inaccessible game UI. Provide persistent labels, text status, keyboard access, reduced motion, and non-color state distinctions.
- First contact never grants authority. Observation and course completion do not authorize live services, credentials, environmental intervention, disclosure, or destructive action.

## Review alarms

Stop a surface-canon pass if it:

- describes a landscape as random, magical, decorative, nonsensical, or naturally occurring without evidence;
- gives the Pilot an occupational answer before repeated input/transformation/output evidence;
- turns a provisional human occupation into a confirmed Builder term;
- lets Teacher copy explain Builder intent, culture, or hidden function;
- implies human ergonomics, prior human contact, a waiting host, or an interface built for the protagonist;
- introduces `we`, `our`, a party, a crew, or a ship as the observing viewpoint;
- makes 2026 human technology the equal or source of the Builder process;
- uses a safe Terminal exercise as proof of Machine approval, consciousness, or full translation;
- leaks hidden canon into chat, accessibility text, logs, tests, or metadata.
