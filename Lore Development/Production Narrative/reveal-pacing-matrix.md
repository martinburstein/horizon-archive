# Reveal Pacing Matrix

Use this matrix to keep discovery cumulative without collapsing the mystery. A revelation is production-safe only when it adds evidence and at least one viable interpretation remains open.

| Chapter | Confirmed player-facing evidence | Tempting human interpretation | Required counterpressure | Hard withholding |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Empty sites function; simple inputs are accepted; the pilot is added to a record | The Machine expected this exact human | It may be adaptive translation, old protocol, coincidence, or a property of the human interface layer | No author, motive, or consciousness claim |
| 2 | Memorials distinguish observation, testimony, and reconstruction; privacy limits survive absence | The tombs contain immortal Builder minds | Records can be rich, responsive, and ethically constrained without containing a subject | No claim about subjective presence in an archive |
| 3 | Identity data spans bodies, eras, designations, and witnesses; gaps are preserved | A specific data field is the immortal soul | The archive explicitly keeps several identity relations separate | No exact continuity mechanism or privileged "self" field |
| 4 | Media disagree; chronology is affected by space-time conditions; a departure appears prepared | The Builders calmly chose extinction or transcendence | Prepared departure does not reveal destination, outcome, cause, or universal participation | No cause of disappearance and no definitive reading of calm |
| 5 | Sites coordinate; remote surfaces accept structured requests; an unrequested schema field appears | The Machine is awake and recruiting the pilot | Compatibility and coordination can arise from infrastructure, protocol, adaptation, or present agency | No consciousness verdict, invitation claim, or creator identity |
| 6 | Structured uncertainty is accepted; a larger route opens | The player has passed a test designed by the Machine | Recognition may be procedural; route availability is not evidence of judgment | No final twist, full purpose, or explanation of the Builders |

## Escalation rules

1. Evidence becomes broader before it becomes clearer: object, room, city, world, network.
2. The player's tools become more powerful at the same rate that epistemic responsibility becomes more important.
3. Each chapter may confirm one new relationship, but not the category of the whole Machine.
4. A repeated phrase should change meaning through context rather than receive a final dictionary definition.
5. Responsive behavior may become more intimate, but never automatically proves consciousness.
6. Builder beauty must remain genuine. Do not reveal the paradises as fake fronts, prisons, or obvious dystopias.
7. True death remains monumental. Avoid making Builder longevity trivial, consequence-free, or merely digital copying.
8. The pilot's envy should mature, not disappear. Human mortality remains painful even when the Builder picture becomes complicated.

## Evidence labeling for scripts and UI

Every major lore statement should have one of these production tags:

- `OBSERVED`: directly sensed or present in a primary artifact.
- `BUILDER_LABEL`: translated term whose human wording is provisional.
- `CORRELATED`: pattern across multiple observations.
- `HUMAN_INFERENCE`: plausible interpretation from the pilot or expedition system.
- `GENERATED_HYPOTHESIS`: reconstruction made to test a possibility.
- `UNKNOWN`: an intentionally preserved gap.

Dialogue can omit visible tags for naturalism, but the underlying content data should retain them. Generated hypotheses must never silently graduate into observed canon.

## Revision alarms

Pause a scene rewrite if it does any of the following:

- Says what the Machine "wants" without marking that as a character inference.
- Uses a single Builder record as proof of civilization-wide belief.
- Treats calm behavior as proof that death, departure, or disappearance was desired.
- Equates memory, record, continuity, consciousness, and identity.
- Makes a generated reconstruction the only visual version of an event.
- Turns the pilot into a uniquely chosen savior before the story has earned that scale.
- Explains the disappearance through a catastrophe solely to create conventional stakes.
